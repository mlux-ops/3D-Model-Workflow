Errors and surprises of the phase, each with cause, fix and status. "Jn" is the journal line. The first sessions' errors were not written here at the time (J2 says they were); they are filled in from the journal by the session that resumed at 16:12.

- J1, the editor (process 16168) vanished between 08:59:52 and 09:00:53 while the path-traced still D2 was rendering. Cause: not known; the log ends inside the render and no crash record was written. Fix: the watcher restarted the editor at 10:25; D2 was rendered again at 10:46 (644 s) with `r.RayTracing.Nanite.Mode` 0. Status: handled; cause open.
- J2, the flat lot ground (`SG_lot_placeholder`) and the far-city apron (`FAR_CityStandIn`) passed through the building's lower level, so a camera there saw gravel 9 cm under the eye. Cause: both are flat sheets made in Phases 4 and 7 without a hole for the basement. - J2, two tool errors while cutting: a selection call returned a pair, not a list; and `post_edit_change` does not exist in the Python interface. Fix: unpack the pair; leave the change notice to `set_editor_property`. Status: fixed.
- J3, the 30 GB free-commit line could not be reached: a freshly started editor with `L_Site_M4` open leaves 27.7 to 28.4 GB free of 79.1 GB. Cause: other programs this session may not close (browsers, the Claude desktop program and its helpers, Windows) hold about 30 GB. Fix (workaround): stills, the animation and packaging run with the line lowered to 26 GB, one at a time, with the memory log every 3 s; each peak is written down (a path-traced still raises the editor by about 10.5 GB; lowest free seen 19.3 GB, no crash). - J5, the first animation test frames were three stops too bright. Cause: a cine camera puts its own aperture (f/2.8 unless set) into the exposure, while the lighting level's exposure is written for f/8. Fix: `seq_build.py` sets the camera's aperture to f/8 and motion blur to 0.2; both sequences were rebuilt and saved at 12:21 and 12:22 and read back. Status: fixed.
- J5, the owner interrupted the phase at 12:30 for Phase 13 (the proposed hotel), which changes eight of the twelve stills. Fix: the eight are re-rendered with the hotel on (J6); the earlier eight are kept in `delivery\stills_prehotel\`. Status: in hand.
