**Obstacle first:** part-way through, every card read zero and a picture failed. Cause: the editor window had been minimized (by what is not known; bringing the editor to the front and then losing the front minimizes it again within seconds on this machine), and a minimized editor draws nothing. Fix: the window is put back on screen without bringing it to the front, and every reading now does this first (`window_restore()` in `tools\wieland_lighting.py`). The zero readings were thrown away and the trials repeated.
**Haze:** the engine's default air is far cleaner than city air (its haze value 0.004 per km means a view of several hundred km). Trials, flat card in open air with the sun still at 140,000 lux (sun and sky / sky alone): haze 0.004: 82,482 / 7,512 lux; 0.02: 80,316 / 8,941; 0.05: 75,112 / 11,817; 0.10: 68,307 / 15,511; 0.15: 62,570 / 18,329; 0.20: 56,254 / 20,452. **Set: 0.05** (with its absorbing part kept in the default proportion, 0.0056), a clear summer day with light haze and a view of roughly 50 km; the direct sun on a card facing it is then about 99,000 lux and the sky about 13,000, which are the textbook values for a clear summer afternoon.
**Sun corrected** from 140,000 to **160,000 lux** so that the flat card stays in range. This set value is the light before the engine's atmosphere and is higher than the real sun above the air (about 130,000 lux), because the engine's air dims the sun more than real air does; what is matched is the light arriving on the card.
**Distance fog:** a height fog `LGT_HeightFog` at density 0.005 (the engine's default 0.02 greyed the far end of the street too much; 0 and 0.005 differ only beyond about 150 m). Sheet: `review\phase8\sky\j4_fog_sheet.jpg`. No ground fog.
**Clouds:** a cloud layer `LGT_Clouds` with the engine's own cloud material, which gives scattered fair-weather clouds. The clouds do not shade the sun (that option of the sun is off), so the sunlight on the building is steady; they drift slowly, so the sky's share varies by about 2,000 lux from minute to minute. Sheet: `review\phase8\sky\j4_cloud_sheet.jpg` (S1, S2, S6, S9).
**Readings at the end** (`review\phase8\brightness_readings_j4.json`): flat card in open sun **91,086 lux** (target 80,000 to 100,000; 89,130 two minutes earlier); sky alone **17,985 lux**; card facing the sun 132,909 lux; flat card on the sidewalk 88,328 lux; sunlit sidewalk concrete **0.09 stop over middle grey** at EV 14.64.
**Cost:** frame time at S1 with fog and clouds on 13.5, 14.8, 13.5 ms and off 14.1, 13.3 ms (4 seconds each, Cinematic quality, editor not the front window): the difference is smaller than the run-to-run spread of this quick method, so the per-part cost is taken from profile M6 instead.
Shade is still deep in the pictures (S9); that is judged against the path-traced ground truth in J8, not here. Saved by name: the lighting level.
