What cannot be closed with lighting settings, with cause: S9, the upper windows of the neighbouring houses of the R15 surroundings are about 4 stops brighter in real time. A ray sent through such a window (script j11_trace.py) crosses the glass and then nothing for 300 m: the neighbouring houses are empty shells, and the real-time picture shows sky through the window where the path-traced picture is dark. The two regions are 2.06 and 2.01 percent of the frame, just over the 2 percent line. The remedy is a dark backing behind the neighbours' windows (a surroundings matter, not a lighting setting). D2 and D3, the rooms behind the large bronze-tinted windows (Vitro Solarban R100 Solarbronze): the path tracer dims the sunlight that passes the tinted glass, the real-time renderer lets it pass at full strength, so the sunlit floor is 1.1 stops brighter and the whole room 20 to 28 percent brighter; and the smoke-glass shades of the Kuzco Trinity pendants are drawn light and milky in real time but dark in the path-traced picture (the 2.8 percent region of D3). Both are questions of how the glass materials are drawn by the two renderers, which belongs to the material work, not to lighting settings. Nothing was saved in the project in this step.
