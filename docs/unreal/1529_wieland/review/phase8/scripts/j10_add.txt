Step 8.8: the same twelve views with the real-time renderer, compared with the ground truth. Each view is rendered through the same route as the ground truth (Movie Render Graph Quick Render, same temporary camera, same fixed exposure, 3840 by 2160), with the real-time renderer at the project's settings as they stand (r.RayTracing.Nanite.Mode 0), after 6 seconds of settling and 64 warm-up frames so that the bounced light has built up. S1 alone first, looked at, then the rest. Output review/phase8/realtime. Then review/phase8/scripts/compare_tiers.py computes per view the mean brightness difference over facade and ground (everything that is not sky and not burnt out) and every region larger than 2 percent of the frame that is off by more than one stop, and writes one side-by-side sheet per view (ground truth, real-time, difference map) to review/phase8/compare. Nothing is saved in the project. Free commit is read first.
