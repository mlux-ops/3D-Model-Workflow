Click a point on the plan to select it.

How to draw

  • Click on the plan to add the next point. Drag a point to move it.
  • To change floors, select the last point on the old floor, set "Next segment" to Stair or Lift, then switch to the new floor and click.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag with the right mouse button (or hold Space) to pan.
  • Delete or Backspace removes the selected point.
  • Save, then press "Request videos". The editor makes the videos on its next check; they appear in delivery\video_paths\ and in the Google Drive delivery folder.
