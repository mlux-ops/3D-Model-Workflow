How this works

Each picture shows the Revit material as exported (left) and the Unreal candidate the agent built (right), each lit from the front, at a grazing angle, and on a ball. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Keep leaves the Revit material as it is. Use candidate switches to the right-hand picture. Rebuild asks the agent to make a better material; say in the note what the finish really is (product, colour, sheen) if you know. Click a pressed button again to undo it. Every material you leave undecided is rebuilt. Every Brizo, Hansgrohe or Kohler finish is researched from the manufacturer and rebuilt, whatever you choose.

Every click is saved at once into material_audit.csv (columns decision and owner_note), which is where the agent reads it, and logged in owner_review\decisions_log.csv. Notes save when you click outside the box. Keyboard: click a card, then press 1, 2 or 3.
