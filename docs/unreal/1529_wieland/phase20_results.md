## Words used here

- **Material instance**: a copy of a parent material with its own settings (colours, window grid); each Phase 17 building has one (`MI_P17_T_<county id>` for the skyline, `MI_P17_Z_<county id>` for Zones 1 and 2), made from the facade parent `M_W1529_P17_Facade`. Phase 20 changes only the instances' settings, never the parent.
- **Linear colour**: the colour numbers the engine uses for lighting; a photograph's sRGB colour is converted to linear before it is set.
- **Confidence**: 1 (pure assumption by building type) to 5 (cladding, colour and window grid all confirmed by images), for how much a building looks like the real one.
- **Work list**: the buildings Phase 20 refines, in the order of how much of the views each fills.
- Other words as in `phase17_results.md` ("Words used here").

## Report (Phase 20)

**What Phase 20 delivered.** The downtown towers seen from the penthouse roof deck were checked against freely licensed photographs from Wikimedia Commons, and 17 material instances (the per-building copies of the facade material) were updated: four Phase 17 assumptions corrected outright (the Waldorf Astoria is warm-grey stone with punched windows, not glass; Two Prudential Plaza granite piers with blue-grey glass; One Chicago light blue glass with silver fins; SoNo dark grey frames), the glass and mullion colours of eight glass towers set from photographs, the Merchandise Mart darker tan, two unnamed towers identified (the former Montgomery Ward catalogue house, cream concrete; a pale grey precast grid tower), and three towers confirmed as they were (Willis Tower, Aon Center, Wolf Point East). Window grids follow each tower's real floor height (height divided by storey count). Only instance settings changed; the facade parent and Phase 17's measured geometry are untouched. The skyline panoramas, stills and the profile were redone: every frame-time line is inside the Phase 9 budget (exterior 95th percentile 14.96 ms, roof deck 14.27 ms, graphics card 10.9 and 10.1 ms, game thread 7.8 and 7.4 ms, video memory 8.8 GB).

These buildings keep their Phase 17 materials (the Cook County Assessor's wall material where it exists, brick otherwise), and every colour remains an assumption.
