# Site gaps: what a station can see for which no evidence was gathered

Written on 2026-11-11 by Phase 2 of [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), step 2.10. Companion to [site_survey.csv](site_survey.csv), which lists what is known, and to [phase2_results.md](phase2_results.md).

By the plan's rule "Evidence over habit", each item here is shown in the scene as a neutral placeholder until evidence arrives, and nothing is filled in with what is typical for the neighbourhood. The site visit of step 2.1 has not been made; it is the owner's, and it would close most of this list. Nothing later in the plan is blocked by it.

**What is known without the visit** (so that this list stays short): the lot, the street's width and kerbs, ground heights, every neighbour's footprint, height and roof form as of spring 2022, tree crowns as of spring 2022 when leafless, and what the plat of survey of August 2025 and the site plan record at the lot lines.

## What is not a gap any more

- **The street's width.** The plan had assumed a 66 ft right-of-way. The plat of survey says 50 ft, and the county parcels and the 2022 ground surface agree. Station S1 is re-set accordingly; see `review\phase2\stations.csv`.
- **The alley's width and paving.** There is no alley.
- **True north.** The lot's front line runs 1.18 degrees west of true north.
