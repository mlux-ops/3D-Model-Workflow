# Errors log — xhigh arm, ELM Formed Hexagon Sand

Every error hit during this build, with cause, fix and status. All dates are 2026-09-26. Entries 7–10 are my own review findings: outputs that were wrong or not faithful enough, which I caught before delivery. Entry 11 is a validation false alarm, logged so the measurement method is clear.

| # | Step / artifact | Observed error | Cause | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `crops_sheet.py` orientation check | Diagnostic sheet came out 972 px wide, over the 800 px viewing limit | 4x zoom on 3 x 80 px crops | Re-ran at 3x (732 px) before opening. Nothing over 800 px was opened | Resolved |
| 2 | `prepare_pools.py` | `AttributeError: 'numpy.ndarray' object has no attribute 'ptp'` | NumPy 2.x removed the `ndarray.ptp` method | Replaced with `np.ptp(arr, axis=0)` | Resolved |
| 3 | `build_maps.py` first run, quilting | Reported re-use of 68 % (Raw) and 77 % (Matte), far above the ~30 % pool deficit | (a) The usage penalty counted a block's own overlap pixels, so contiguous continuation from the source was penalised and mostly rejected. (b) Canvas corners outside the hexagon were quilted, spending pool pixels | The penalty now counts only what a block adds beyond its overlaps. Blocks outside the hexagon are skipped. A pixel counts as re-used only when a different placement (face, translation) copies it. An exact duplicate count per output pixel was added | Resolved |
| 4 | `Color.png` v1, Matte face near (3997, 1049) | Two short 60° dark lines inside a Matte face: render joint lines copied into the quilt (ghost joints) | The render lattice's column phase was fitted 8.6 px off (x0 463.0 vs true 454.4) by a 1D comb correlation. Pools rebuilt. `verify.py` gained a ghost-joint detector: max inside Matte/Raw faces 5.5 vs 10.5 on joints, and the top hits were inspected and are Raw crevices | Resolved, re-reviewed |
| 5 | `prepare_chiseled.py` | `ValueError: Invalid number of FFT data points (0)` | The crop of Chiseled face −1,6 (centre x ≈ 181 px) started at a negative index, which gave an empty slice | Padded the source arrays by the crop half-size, with the padding outside every mask | Resolved |
