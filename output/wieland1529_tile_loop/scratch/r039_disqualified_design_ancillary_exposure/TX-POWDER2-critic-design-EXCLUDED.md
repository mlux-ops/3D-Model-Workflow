# R039 independent DESIGN critique — TX-POWDER2

Seat: DESIGN. Strict-blind source-only assessment. Two physical instances, 009A and 009B. Scored **only the ONE DRY W1 vanity wall**, with existing wood floors retained. The Hansgrohe Tecturis E **73072341 Brushed Black Chrome** is deck-mounted, and the basin is **UNSELECTED**. Five separate 1–5 **integer** criterion ratings per letter; unweighted mean rounded to one decimal with HALF_UP. No other seat, incumbent identity, prior score or current letter mapping used.

### Design-seat conclusion

**A 3.8; B 3.6.** A offers the stronger contrasting powder-room character with known Brushed Black Chrome, while B offers an easier warm palette tied to wood and the first TX powder. Both require basin and mirror-interface evidence. These are **independent design-seat results only**; no four-seat weighting or champion decision.
