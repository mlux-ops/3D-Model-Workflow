Do not imply A is a qualified replacement without genuine independent scoring.
3. **TX-BATH3 (BNX; tub versus walk-in UNSELECTED):** A uses a deep-blue gloss wall with neutral floor; B uses Elm Emerald gloss (stronger Old Town OT04 qualitative resonance, **not** identical SKU) plus warmer Pienza floor.
