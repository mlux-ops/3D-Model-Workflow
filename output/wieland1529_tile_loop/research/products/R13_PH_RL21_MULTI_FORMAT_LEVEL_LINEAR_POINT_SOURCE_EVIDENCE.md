Require exact order-suffix/production tile, physical chip including carrier and setting configuration, product specification revision and written Brizo waterproof/grate approval. PH Primary R013's factory **solid-grate** study bypasses the decorative tile insert regardless of this conflict, but not pan-to-drain or leveling requirements.
