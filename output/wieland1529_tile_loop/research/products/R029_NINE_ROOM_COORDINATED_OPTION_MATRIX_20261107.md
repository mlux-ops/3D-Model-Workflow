## Reading the matrix and hard-gate discipline

- **W** = wet tub/shower wall; **N** = wet niche face/back with recess, return, sill and exposed-edge acceptance still conditional; **L** = bathroom LEVEL floor; **P** = conditional POINT shower pan; **D** = dry powder vanity wall. **CEILING** and **LINEAR PAN** are **N/A in all nine rooms**. A manufacturer shower-WALL listing does **not** prove ceiling approval or finished niche-edge construction. A FLOOR listing alone does **not** prove shower-PAN permission. **L and P require individually published wet DCOF at least 0.42**, and P additionally requires manufacturer Shower Floor permission. A manufacturer's numeric field here is its published product-page statement, not an independently received raw laboratory report.
- The products in the **A/B** columns are **design proposals only**. A subsequent column supplies three **distinct product lines per actual surface**; different colors of one Tones line or different sizes of Luxe Sand are **not** counted as separate product lines. Products with unknown application permission appear as held comparisons, not as gate-passing third options. All recommended A/B LEVEL fields are large format (24 in nominal side); the only potential P is a manufacturer-listed large-format tile subject to installer-cut geometry.
- Price precedence: Elm same-job quote of **2026-09-04** (Gmail thread **1a068e80c92c255f**, recorded in **inputs/TRADE_QUOTES.md**) exceeds earlier-project pricing; F&D prices are dated **2026-11-06/07 public product pages** at **non-Chicago selected stores**; MSI unquoted prices are **price on request**. Porcelanosa published Bottega prices are genuine public price leads, but its exact-SKU wet DCOF and installation permissions are **UNKNOWN**, so it is **not** a qualified wet floor, wall or pan alternative.
- All historic **gross** quantities below already include original **15% waste exactly once**. No additional 15% is applied. Area-rate screens are **material tile only** and use those provisional historical gross rows; they do not certify independent net wall/niche quantities, carton economics, Chicago freight, tax, trim, waterproofing, grout, mortar, labor or delivered cost. Count the physical niche only if confirmed and credit overlapping wall area once it is measured. A cut-heavy P will need its **own measured net area and documented waste decision** after the owner confirms the shower program; P quantities here are **null**.
- The model is correctly identified by the owner: native **25-006 1527 N Wieland** is the building also called **1529 Wieland**. Every physical room dimension below remains prior/drawing-derived or assumed, **live Revit verification false**, and room/finish element receipts are absent. A favorable conceptual layout is not an approved geometry gate, especially if an exposed cut could fall under **3 in**.
- Old Town comps OT01–OT05 are the primary market set at roughly the **$2.4M–$3.2M** comparison band; OT01–OT04 represent one building/design group and no verified wet-bath SKU/grout/metal can be inferred from those listings.
