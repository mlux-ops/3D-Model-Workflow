## 1. The critical evidence distinction

Use four distinct columns/claims in every supplier approval:

1. **Intended application** — maker's product-specific **Wet Areas** approval for wet **wall**, or explicit **Shower Floor** listing for pan, or wet-level floor application for bath/WC. "Floors", "Walls" or a collection-wide label alone must **not** be transferred to a different relief, glaze, finish, size or SKU.
2. **Published performance threshold** — Daltile Portfolio's application table says **Target DCOF Wet ≥0.42**. This is the target attached to a *use class*, **not an actual measured performance of each PF07/PF05/PF09 12×24 or 2×2 format**. Exact SKU measured result = `null` and strict floor gate **FAIL / NOT CLEARED** pending data.
3. **Published product-specific reported bound** — Glazzio Surfaces individual HNC6615/HNC6715 tables say **DCOF WET >0.50**, while the manufacturer's legacy Glazzio Tiles exact item pages say **DCOF >0.42** (see conflicting-source finding below). Both are inequalities **greater than** a published bound; neither is an *exact measured point value* (e.g., do not invent "0.51"), lab-method report ID, date, surface condition or signed certified test. Do not silently change prior board results; classify as **promising but evidence reconciliation / lab report HOLD**.
4. **Project release gate** — [BOARD.md](../../board/BOARD.md) requires shower and bath floors a *published numeric wet DCOF at least 0.42* and interprets unknown as fail. The project has chosen strict exact SKU/finish documentation; this R05 manufacturer audit **does not independently adjudicate the board's earlier decisions**. Wall listing alone does not clear the separate floor gate.

**Standards nuance:** [Daltile's AcuTest explanation](https://www.daltile.com/why-daltile/industry-standards/dcof-slip-resistance-testing-reading-test-results) describes ANSI A326.3 measurements, recognizes **≥0.42 wet** for *level interior spaces expected to be walked wet*, says tile is not "slip proof," and emphasizes drainage, contaminants, footwear and maintenance. **ANSI A326.3's shower-floor suitability is not reduced to a universal single number in every condition; the SHB board's ≥0.42 shower requirement is a stricter *project release rule***. Record both the **maker-listed shower application** and the **project's numeric evidence** separately; a product can have a maker shower-floor YES but still be held by project verification.

## 3. ANSI/TCNA installation references — scope, not invented tolerances

| Topic | Controlling public reference and technically defensible check |
|---|---|
| **Codes / current publication identity** | [TCNA ANSI standards status](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/ansi-standards/) lists **ANSI A108.02** workmanship (current listed 2023) and **ANSI A108.01** substrate preparation (2026). The [2026 TCNA Handbook](https://tcnatile.com/products/publications/2026-tcna-handbook-for-ceramic-glass-and-stone-tile-installation/) is the method-selection reference. **Actual TCNA method/section, edition, membrane family, jurisdiction approval and approved assembly are `null`** for every room until detailed; a handbook listing is not a stamped local determination. |
| **Wet backing and waterproof membrane** | [TCNA Shower FAQ B421/B422](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/showers/) says compatible **membrane/backer combinations are declared by their actual manufacturers**; not every backing (especially gypsum) is universally accepted. [Schluter tiled-shower systems primer](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/article-tiled-shower-waterproofing) distinguishes traditional liner/clamping-drain assemblies from bonded waterproofing and **drain bonding flange** systems. Require a **single compatible system design** under the actual Brizo/Delta drain; do not splice incompatible drain/membrane details. The primer identifies **ANSI A118.10** for bonded waterproof membrane criteria; product approval not inferred. |
| **Substrate flatness and lippage** | [TCNA Lippage FAQ](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/lippage/) explains warpage, surface plane, joint width and raking light influence lippage. Record **actual measured substrate flatness `null`** until survey; do not assert an unverified LFT substrate meets ANSI tolerances or quote an unverified tolerance as a field result. |
| **Large-format offsets and grout** | [TCNA Grout FAQ](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/grout/) references ANSI A108.02 4.3.8 joint straightness and manufacturer/dimensional allowances; there is **no universal grout width** suitable for every product. Favor **zero-offset aligned 24×48 and 12×24 mock-up**. For offset patterns with a long edge over 18 in, **check the manufacturer's limit/1/3-offset guidance; do not treat 50% offset as automatically permitted** ([Custom Building Products offset bulletin archived in R04](https://www.custombuildingproducts.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/tb87-grout-joint-width.pdf)). Installed joint must honor actual lot/caliber and specified mortar. |
| **Movement and interfaces** | [TCNA Movement Joint Placement FAQ](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/placement/) points to Handbook **EJ171**; plan flexible movement joints at changes of plane, restraining boundaries, tub rims, wet floor transitions and wood thresholds, not rigid grout in every interface. **Spacing, width and sealant SKU remain `null` until design**. |
| **Point versus linear drain** | [Schluter linear-drain page](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/showers/shower_with_linear_drain) demonstrates why **wall vs center drain placement changes slope/tray geometry**; it is **not** a Brizo assembly detail. A **4-in. square point drain** generally requires multiple sloped planes or another engineered positive-fall arrangement, better served by a **small mosaic** after plan/section. A 48-in. linear drain does **not prove** one-plane geometry without actual installed wall location and curb/threshold. **Do not invent an actual slope rate**, pan SF, or checked curb height before the design is selected and measured. |
| **Tile/grout waterproof misconception** | [TCNA Ceramic Tile FAQ](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/ceramic-tile/) emphasizes grout joints **are not waterproof** and wet-area safety depends on the substrate/assembly, not merely the selected tile. Require waterproof continuity behind tile at walls, pan, drain throat, niche, curb, thresholds and service penetrations. |
| **Niche/bench drainage** | [Schluter niche detail guidance](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/faq/shower-system-kerdiboard-shower-niche-shelf-install) supports a **positively drained niche shelf** with compatible assembly; this cannot be converted to an invented numeric pitch for every niche. A **primary shower niche is expressly NOT in scope** in PH/TX Primary, regardless of historical unpriced row. |
