It must NOT be promoted to an overhead shower ceiling simply because a wet wall is allowed. This confirms the importance of exact product-level Wall/Ceiling identity.

To name an acceptable PH/TX ceiling tile under owner hard gates, require one independently documented **allowed-vendor SKU/variant** suitable for wet shower **ceiling/overhead** use (not merely wet wall), nominal/actual size, thickness and edge, compatible complete overhead mortar/substrate/waterproof assembly with current maker installation instructions, proposed finish coordinated with existing BNX + selected Cortar WALL and F&D LEVEL/PAN, manufacturer source URL and dated current price or **PRICE ON REQUEST**, and original room ceiling quantity when physically verified. The three distinct ceiling contenders must be assessed as **CEILING**, not WALL, and cannot be silently inferred from wall product records. Review of an actual maker ceiling listing is still **OPEN**; if maker data cannot verify, remain BLOCKED.
