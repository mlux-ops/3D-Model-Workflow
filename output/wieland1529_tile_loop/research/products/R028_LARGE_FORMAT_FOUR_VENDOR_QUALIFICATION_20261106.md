## Installation, grout, drainage and availability controls

- F&D product pages list 1/16, 1/8 and 3/16-in grout options; 1/8 in is a layout starting point, not a room-specific approval. MSI exact product pages do not publish grout width or thickness for Murcia/Cordova; leave those fields UNKNOWN. Material Bank lists 1/8-in for Bottega, but that is not a Porcelanosa-USA sheet.
- The owner directs the installer to cut and slope large-format tile in the pan. F&D Luxe Sand 101317949 and Riviera Oat 101487775 have product-page Shower Floor YES and wet DCOF >=0.42; they are the strongest large-format PAN options in this comparison. Actual drain centerline, substrate, cuts and visible edge slivers under approximately 3 in still require measured native/field geometry.
- MSI Murcia/Cordova and Porcelanosa Bottega are not promoted to PAN PASS from a generic DCOF or residential-floor rating. Their exact shower-floor application permission is not stated in the available product evidence.
- Elm Cortar 24x48 import: 10–12 weeks, 50% deposit, 300 SF aggregated import minimum, per 2026-09-04 same-job quote. Tones quote lists 6.50 SF/carton and FOB FL/IL/NJ/TX; no product-specific lead time is stated. F&D pages provide online/store ordering and non-Chicago stock contexts, not Chicago inventory. MSI pages expose Check Inventory but no live Chicago quantity/ETA in this review. Porcelanosa historic NJ-to-IL weekly truck terms are from other projects and are not a current product quote or availability promise.
- No samples, cart additions, quote requests, vendor contact, emails or orders were made. Price-on-request items remain owner-only quote requests.
