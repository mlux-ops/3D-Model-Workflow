**Important maker brochure version discrepancy:** the **older** RL20/21/23 official brochure under `digitalassets.daltile.com/.../core-fundamentals-prime/DAL_CoreFundamentals_Brochure_Prime.pdf` reports **linear-drain target wet DCOF ≥0.60 with suitable mosaic checkmark**, whereas the **April 2026 manufacturer-branded brochure** [Core Fundamentals PRIME](https://s7d9.scene7.com/is/content/daltile/DAL_CoreFundamentals_Brochure_Primepdf) reports **linear-drain target ≥0.42 with suitable mosaic checkmark**. These are *two documented public versioned maker declarations with different application targets*, not measured tile-specific test results and not guaranteed to apply identically to all colors, finishes, cuts or SKU revisions. **Do not discard either version, invent a measured ≥0.60, or silently adopt the lower 2026 application target as a certified wet-DCOF value.** Resolve the current exact-product technical sheet and testing method via an authorized procurement channel before any specification promotion.
