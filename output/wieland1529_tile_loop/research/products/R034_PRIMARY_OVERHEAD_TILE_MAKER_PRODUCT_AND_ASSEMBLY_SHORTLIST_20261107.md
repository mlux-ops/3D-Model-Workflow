## The substantive finding and how to clear it

Schluter explicitly supports *tile assemblies on shower ceilings*: a mechanically secured KERDI-BOARD ceiling with waterproofed joints and fastener penetrations, compatible thin-set mortar and temporary support for tile during curing. This provides a real **system-side** construction pathway. It does **not** give Elm, MSI, Porcelanosa or Floor & Decor permission to install any particular field tile **overhead in a wet shower**. No exact tile SKU among the manufacturer listings below explicitly states overhead wet-shower ceiling suitability. Consequently **zero complete product + ceiling-assembly pairings have been proven**. Neither room's ceiling can be marked qualified from the current sources.

The Porcelanosa article [*The Timeless Beauty of Bathrooms with Mosaic Tiles*](https://store.porcelanosa.com/uk/get-the-look/bathroom-mosaic-tile-ideas), section **Bathroom Ceilings**, explicitly describes a mosaic-tiled coved/vaulted ceiling **over a bathtub or shower enclosure** and also discusses flat ceilings. This is affirmative *design guidance*, but it contains **no product SKU, ceiling loading limit, wet-overhead installation schedule or tested attachment detail**. Its illustrated/named mosaics are not automatically approved for this exposure. In particular the identifiable Porcelanosa **Herringbone Mystic Beige 100351893** on the manufacturer's [own UK product page](https://store.porcelanosa.com/uk/herringbone-mystic-beige-26x28x0-6) is classified for bathroom walls/glass wall, with **no shower-ceiling use** listed. Do not convert the article into an individual tile's technical warranty or permit.

## Real assembly pathway: manufacturer documents, not an invented trade detail

1. **Load-bearing framing and substrate.** Schluter's [KERDI-BOARD official installation instructions](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/Building-Panels/KERDI-BOARD-Panels/Schluter-KERDI-BOARD/p/KB1512203050) specify minimum **1/2 in (12.5 mm) board on framing 16 in o.c.**, or **3/4 in (19 mm) board on framing 24 in o.c.**, installed directly to wood/metal framing with corresponding KERDI-BOARD-ZT washers and screws (wood coarse thread, metal self-tapping). Minimum screw embedment **3/4 in wood** / **3/8 in metal**; **maximum 6 in o.c. fastener spacing on ceilings** (**12 in** is the *wall* maximum). Use centered supports at abutting board edges. Field verify ceiling framing, orientation, strength, backing, penetrations, and the **combined dead load of actual tile + mortar + grout + board** before specification. Schluter's separate [shower-system details](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/showers/residential_steam_showers) explicitly address a waterproof tiled ceiling, particularly where an enclosed/steam shower is applicable; **the project has not been established as a steam shower**.
2. **Continuous shower waterproofing.** Seal each KERDI-BOARD joint, corner and fastener penetration using **KERDI-BAND with minimum 2 in overlap** and the specified compatible thin-set; keep wall-to-ceiling membrane continuity. Seal the BNX **ceiling-mounted rainhead arm 899110-12-BNX** and any overhead lights/vents with a documented compatible penetration method and details maintaining the waterproofing plane; do not rely on the shower-arm escutcheon alone. The exact field penetration section is not currently verified.
3. **Tile bond.** Schluter's [official KERDI tile mortar FAQ](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/faq/shower-system-kerdi-thin-set-mortar-type-ceramic-stone-tile) identifies **Schluter SET, ALL-SET, FAST-SET, or ANSI A118.1 unmodified thin-set** for ceramic/stone tile on KERDI and notes that ceiling applications **may require temporary support until mortar cures**. The [KERDI-BOARD page](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/Building-Panels/KERDI-BOARD-Panels/Schluter-KERDI-BOARD/p/KB1512203050) directs selection of a notch matching tile format and back-buttering as necessary for full coverage. **Premixed mastic must not be substituted**; [Schluter's FAQ](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/faq/shower-system-kerdi-premixed-thin-set-mortar-tile) disallows premixed adhesives in KERDI shower work. For the glass mesh mosaic, neither this ordinary ceramic/stone mortar guidance nor the generic ceiling waterproof detail establishes a compatible specific glass-tile bond.
4. **Gravity / installation sequencing.** The installer must submit a **tile-size- and weight-specific temporary overhead support/shoring method** that stays until the selected mortar has developed adequate strength, maintains coverage and flatness and does not pierce the waterproof plane. Do not assume a "non-sag" label, a spot-bonded panel, grout, or tape is a complete engineered load path. Schluter's material explicitly warns about glass tile incompatibility and moisture-sensitive/resin-backed stone; the manufacturer's adhesive/backing data matters.
5. **Perimeter/penetrations and commissioning.** Maintain a movement-accommodating wall/ceiling change-of-plane joint; compatible sealant or a specified movement-joint profile after confirming the correct tile thickness. Test waterproofing before field tile per system and local requirements; Schluter [states a minimum 24-hour wait after membrane installation before water testing](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/faq/kerdiboard-wait-before-tile-install). Confirm fan/lighting/ceiling-mounted rainhead penetrations, gravity load and framing **for each physical shower**.

**Limits of assembly evidence:** Schluter establishes a *potential* overhead ceiling support/waterproofing/tile method. It has **not verified** any of the five specific manufacturer SKUs/ELM unnamed size-finish combinations, the actual backing load path, bond to each backing/mounting mesh, or project-specific surface configuration. Therefore **assembly pathway documented = yes; complete named product-and-assembly technical qualification = no**.
