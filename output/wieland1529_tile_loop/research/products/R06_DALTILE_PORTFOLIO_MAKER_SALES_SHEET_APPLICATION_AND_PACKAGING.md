## Packaging arithmetic — illustration, not an order

If a future separately accepted PF09/PF07/PF05 **2x2 point-drain mosaic pan** has verified **14.6 SF gross including the one original 15% waste**, then the series-level carton illustration would be **ceil(14.6 / 24.00) = 1 carton**, nominal **24 SF coverage / 9.4 SF extra over illustration**. In contrast a hypothetical **25.6 SF gross** of matching **12x24 field** would be **ceil(25.6 / 17.02) = 2 cartons**, nominal **34.04 SF coverage / 8.44 SF extra**. **This 25.6 +14.6 =40.2 gross PH Bath4 split is an unresolved geometry example, not a verified owner-approved 35/27 SF reconciliation**; it is never an order. Current exact SKU/finish/color pack and shade/lot as supplied, sourcing price, freight, stock and lead remain `null`.

**Non-overlap with original R05 Glazzio alternative:** Glazzio HNC6715 is **4.85 SF/five-sheet box** and HNC6615 is **9.69 SF/five-tile box**; never substitute those package yields for Portfolio. Glazzio's newer `>0.50` and legacy `>0.42` maker numeric bounds are separate evidence and not equivalent to a Daltile measured result or Daltile's **linear-drain printed target ≥0.60**.
