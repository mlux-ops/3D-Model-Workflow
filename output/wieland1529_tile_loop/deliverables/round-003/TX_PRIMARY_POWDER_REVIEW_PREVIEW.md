## TX Primary — full shower and ceiling

The historic v4 palette combines ELM Cortar Clay flat 24×48 Soft wet walls/ceiling with an unmeasured Clay Ribbed relief feature, plus a blended 24×48 Soft level-floor and 12×24 Anti-Slip shower-pan allowance. **Clay Ribbed is not Wet Areas-listed**, and the exact wet DCOF of both carried floor variants has not been established. The historic **134.4-SF wall/ceiling** and **116.1-SF blended floor** allowances include 15% waste; their quoted material cost is **$1,999.60/unit** before other trades.

The additional concept uses **flat Clay only** in the wet enclosure and Daltile Portfolio **Cream PF07 12×24 Matte** on the level bathroom/WC floor with **PF07 2×2 Matte** on the shower pan. That separates level field from the drain pan instead of forcing a long rigid tile across multiple slopes. However, the manufacturer states **Target Wet DCOF ≥0.42**, not an exact measured number for either SKU; **both are held** under the project's strict acceptance test. The 48-in Brizo linear drain remains unchanged, with actual drain position, pan slope, curb/threshold and waterproofing unknown. No primary-bath niche is in the governing scope; do not add one.

The older quote's floor split is internally inconsistent by **$0.08** when separately extending 87.64 SF Soft and 28.46 SF Anti-Slip at displayed rates; preserve the carried **$905.58 floor row** and do not treat the split as a renewed supplier quote. No complete landed cost exists for the Daltile mosaic.

## TX Powder — Haze Camel/Wheat

Dry vanity wall only; retain **wood flooring**. Source scope is **31.8 SF gross/unit**, 15% waste already included; ELM Haze Camel/Wheat blended historical material price **$379.37/unit**. The refinement tests a controlled **70% Camel / 30% Wheat** arrangement, matte stacked vertical small-formats and warm grout rather than an unquantified blend; its per-color box allocation and true supplier rate are not separately confirmed. Verify the wall-mounted Hansgrohe faucet rough, decorative wall landing, mirror and grout layout with the approved plumbing schedule. No floor slip-resistance gate applies to the existing wood floor.

The other documented concept, **ELM Vera Forma Earth Lines 2×10 relief**, carries **$8.20/SF** from a Penthouse Powder quote; the **TX quote is absent** and cannot be transferred as an approved rate. Keep it as a cost/design research option, not a new selection.

## TX Powder 2 — Indigo Edge vanity wall

Dry feature wall only with **wood floor**. Source tile is **ELM Vera Forma Indigo Edge 6×6 Glossy Relief**, **40.5 SF gross/unit at $8.20/SF = $332.10/unit** before carton rounding and installed costs. The refinement aligns a balanced grid around an optional smooth, measured backsplash/mirror mounting zone rather than leaving the protruding relief beneath every penetration. That smooth panel is **unselected**, with material, extent, cost, bonding/cleanability and perimeter edge detail **null**; if used it must subtract existing Indigo field tile, not add extra wall area.

**Hansgrohe Tecturis E 73072341 Brushed Black Chrome is a deck-mounted faucet.** It does not justify a wall-mounted spout/rough-in or an invented sink. Powder 2's **basin is unselected**, so countertop, faucet clearance, mirror and any smooth landing must await owner/layout confirmation.
