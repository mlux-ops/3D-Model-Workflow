## Correction requiring design coordinator attention

Daltile's detailed Portfolio sales sheet distinguishes **standard center-drain shower floor (mosaic suitable; target wet DCOF ≥0.42)** from **linear-drain shower floor (target wet DCOF ≥0.60, with no suitable checkmark)**. This is not itself a measured SKU slip test or a categorical ban on linear shower installations, but **a missing manufacturer application clearance for the exact planned PF07 2×2 linear-drain shower pan**. The general Daltile color pages' shower-floor YES label must not be treated as independent explicit authorization of the linear-drain scheme. Both PH and TX Primary Brizo **48-in linear** showers remain **HOLD** for actual linear-drain suitability **and** exact floor/pan tested wet DCOF. No primary shower niche.

The **2×2 Portfolio mosaic is glazed ceramic, not the 12×24 ColorBody porcelain field tile**. Maker series-level coverage: **2×2 dot-mounted glazed ceramic: 24.00 SF/carton, 12 sheets (nominal 12×24 sheet), 1/4-in thickness**; **12×24 ColorBody porcelain: 17.02 SF/carton, 9 pieces, 5/16-in thickness**. Finished field-to-pan elevation and mortar/membrane need an explicit drawn setting-bed transition. Carton yields are manufacturer-series observations, not confirmed exact PF05/PF07/PF09 current lot or buy.
