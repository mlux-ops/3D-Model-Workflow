## Use of this book and the 13 sample-kit cards

This book makes the prior R04 source extents actionable as **layout studies**, without silently approving materials, measuring unmeasured openings, assigning tile orders or promoting candidates. **Dimension notation:** each room repeats the exact source **length in feet** and **tiled height in feet**; parenthetic **inches are the rounded R04 display** (not more precise as-built inches). A nominal module calculation deliberately **excludes grout, actual calibrated tile dimensions, trim thickness, waterproofing and openings**. For example, a centered 24-in-width vertical 24x48 panel on a theoretical 60-in-wide wall may show 24+24+6+6 in; those 6-in ends are **conceptual**, not approved cut sizes. Final cuts are recomputed from actual tile lot and wall/valve elevations. **Door/window/sill position, mirror limits, valve rough centerlines, glass, exposed-edge quantity, stock trim lengths, room-specific owner budget and Chicago lead/stock = null until verified.**

**Design sample-key:** W = tile wall field; A = accent/niche tile; F = level floor tile; P = slope-following shower-pan tile; G = grout/movement-joint finish; E = exposed edge/transition/profile; H = exact selected fixture metal or ceramic reference; S = selected sink/vanity/wood reference where present. A **named manufacturer product/color/format is not automatically an orderable SKU**: where individual manufacturer order codes have not been documented, the sample line says **order SKU null**. **PF07/PF05/PF09 are Daltile color codes, not proof of complete supplier order codes.** ELM source WT/FT/AT codes are SHB schedule identifiers, not manufacturer SKUs. A kit is a **comparison checklist**, not a sample request: **no physical samples, sample photographs, rendered images, vendor forms or orders were created.**

**Finish hierarchy and test evidence:** exact selected plumbing finishes remain fixed; wet walls need the named **individual manufacturer's Wet Areas** listing. **Sapphire 2x20 Glossy is wet-listed** ([maker source](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/sapphire-tones)); Bone/Clay **flat Soft**, Tones Sandstone/Copper/Emerald and Soho Black have individual product-use evidence. **Opal, Bone Reed/Ribbed, Clay Ribbed and Clara Caviar have no verified product-specific wet-wall listing for the proposed application** and must not be assumed acceptable based on another finish. Tiled bath **level floors and individual shower pans each require separate exact-SKU tested wet DCOF**. Daltile **Target wet DCOF >=0.42** on PF07/PF05/PF09 is a stated application target **not an exact measured result**; both field and pan remain **FAIL** where used. Glazzio HNC6615 12x24 and HNC6715 2x2 hex individually state **wet DCOF >0.50** on maker pages, but raw point test/certificate, chosen palette, approval, price and delivery remain **null**; they are **research-only cards**, not retroactive Round004 substitutions ([R04 shortlist](../integrated-r04/TESTED_FLOOR_CANDIDATE_SHORTLIST_R04.md)).

**Material quantity rule:** all 2026-09-03 ELM historical gross SF already include **15% source waste**; **do not apply 15% twice**. A niche's occupied wall face and additional backs/jambs/sills require one measured reconciliation, never an unverified second full-field allowance. The **PH WC 15-SF and TX WC 14-SF source areas are already represented within the respective primary quoted floor lines**, without another WC order. Historical dollars are **not** a current supplier quotation, owner room allowance or installed construction cost. **All approved trim LF, actual stick counts, pan SF allocations, carton orders and pricing revisions = null**. See [R04 trim detail schedule](../integrated-r04/13_ROOM_TRIM_TRANSITION_AND_CUT_CONTROL_R04.md), [owner decision cards](../integrated-r04/13_ROOM_OWNER_DECISION_CARDS_R04.md), and [original source rollup](../integrated-r04/PROJECT_MATERIAL_ROLLUP_R04.md).
