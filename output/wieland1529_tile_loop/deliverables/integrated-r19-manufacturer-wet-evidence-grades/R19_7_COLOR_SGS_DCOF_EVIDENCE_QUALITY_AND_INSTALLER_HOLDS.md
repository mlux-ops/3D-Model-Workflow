Zia Tile's official [Cantera installation and specification PDF](https://support.ziatile.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/25221619136148) contains an ANSI A326.3 color-specific numerical DCOF table credited to third-party **SGS TEC Services**. The published A326.3 color table does **NOT state the actual wet test fluid or test condition** and does **NOT supply the underlying original laboratory specimen certificate/report ID**, test date, exact tested format/SKU/finish, sealer, wax or final installed surface condition. Strict 1529 original raw wet LEVEL DCOF lab remains **FAIL/NULL for applicable tiled bathrooms**; independently applicable LINEAR or SQUARE POINT shower PAN raw wet lab remains **FAIL/NULL**. DRY powder wood floors require no tiled bath LEVEL or shower PAN tests (**N/A**); both WC rooms inherit their respective Primary LEVEL test once; actual Duravit TUB-only rooms have no tiled shower PAN (**N/A**).

## Why the material is NOT automatically interchangeable with the chosen 12x24 bath floor

- Cantera is natural volcanic lava stone requiring experienced installer and controlled grout, cleaning and field sealing. It is not a porcelain drop-in for original ELM Cortar Soft or proposed Daltile Prime.
- The official maker nominal thickness is **3/4 in**; 24x12 nominal tile carton covers 9.70 SF and weighs 72 lb for 5 pieces, versus thinner baseline 3/8-in nominal Daltile 12x24 alternatives. Finished floor elevation, load, thresholds, tub flange/door clearance, trowel/build-up, plumbing rough and substrate capacity require architect/structural/installer review.
- The manufacturer describes 15–20% cut/breakage overage for its natural stone, with more for appearance sorting. This is NOT a new allowance applied to historic original gross: the original 2026-09-03 tile source gross already contains 15% once. A newly chosen cantera design would require true verified NET 1529 area and a separately owner/GC approved new cut allowance ONCE, never two full flooring orders or double historic waste.
- Caution: Maker installation includes pre-grout seal and interior matte top-coat protective wax, but the DCOF chart DOES NOT identify which sealer and service state the exact tested tile used. Sealed wet dynamic friction can differ materially. Demand test record for exact final installed condition, rather than assuming 0.54-0.63 represents an independently established wet, post-wax or project-installed condition.
- Four-plane square POINT and 48in LINEAR sloped PANs require independent appropriate small-format pan tile, compliant slopes and separately tested result. A 12x24 heavy stone format should **NOT** be assigned to any POINT/LINEAR shower PAN merely because a color has a published A326.3 numerical DCOF table with wet condition unspecified; those schemes remain completely unapproved.
