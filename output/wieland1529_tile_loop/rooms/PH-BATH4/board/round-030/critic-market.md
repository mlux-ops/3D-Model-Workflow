No owner veto. Gross-source material screen **$1,092.98/copy** uses same-job Elm 2026-09-04 trade rates and **Kirkwood GA F&D $3.99/SF**; it is not local landed cost or a validated takeoff ([trade quotes](../../../../inputs/TRADE_QUOTES.md)).

**confidence: medium.** Niche photograph and final room lighting unavailable; comp wet image supports character only.
