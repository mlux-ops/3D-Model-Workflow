- **POINT_PAN_RL21_HEX_RAW_NUMERIC_DCOF_FAIL:** Distinct **1.5-in hex Matte** is maker shower-floor suitable as a study, but same **Target ≥0.42** is **not a measured PAN wet DCOF**. - **POINT_DRAIN_MEMBRANE_SLOPE_HOLD:** [Brizo BT062415-BNX](../../plumbing.json), drain throat, liner/membrane, 4-plane finished fall, curb, splash threshold and flood-test/assembly compatibility are unverified against [geometry](../../geometry.json). Do not assume a linear pan or tub.
- **35_VS_27_SOFT_VS_ANTISLIP_AND_SCOPE_HOLD:** Historic floor **40.2 SF gross** is **ONE pooled LEVEL+POINT PAN** line, original **15% waste included**, notwithstanding owner 35 versus prior 27 SF and source Soft versus AntiSlip. Current owner cap, vendor quote, lot and purchase release null. No demonstrated HIGH fixture clash or quantified 125%-budget failure.

**confidence:** **medium** for wet wall/niche maker evidence and deliberate Black Onyx palette; **low** for pan dimensions, floor testing, lighting and actual installed waterproofing.

C retains compelling relief imagery but fails two wet-wall/niche permissions and separate LEVEL/PAN testing; neither option is ready for owner/procurement release.
