# Round 012 — independent interior DESIGN critique — TX-PRIMARY

**Scope:** Blind neutral letters **B/C** only. Five independently assessed DESIGN criteria on a 1–5 scale; seat score = their simple arithmetic mean rounded to one decimal. The board records design weight 0.25, but this report applies **no weighting**, computes no aggregate and assigns no source/revised roles.

**Evidence boundary:** [Selected plumbing](../../plumbing.json) includes **Brizo BT0734816-BNX 48-in Black Onyx LINEAR drain** with **BR73480 rough**, Black Onyx Roccesco fittings, a separately selected **Victoria + Albert Terrassa freestanding tub**, and a descoped body-spray row that remains excluded. **NO PRIMARY NICHE**; level bath floor includes the **shared TX WC**. The [owner price band](../../../../state/OWNER_FEEDBACK.md) is **TX $2.7M**. [Comps OT01–OT05, LP01–LP03 and GC01](../../../../research/comps/COMPS.md) document premium bath *programs*, not any authenticated tile pattern or color from a bathroom photograph. The active three-room TX palette comparison is bounded to these current R012 designs (Clay/Black Onyx Primary, Copper/Chrome Bath2, Charcoal/Matte White Bath4); the full 13-room finish coherence cannot be certified from the authorized room set.
