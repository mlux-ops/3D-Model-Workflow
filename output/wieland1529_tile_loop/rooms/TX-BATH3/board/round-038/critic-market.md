Seat mean: (4 + 3 + 4 + 3) / 4 = 3.5 (HALF_UP)

top_fix: Show the blue field, beige niche, matte floor and Black Onyx hardware together in a full-room view so the saturated wall reads intentional at the room's actual coverage.

gate_flags: No listed product gate failure. La Belle Blue and Purity list Shower Walls YES and Shower Floors NO, and are used on vertical faces only. Luxe Sand lists Bathroom Floor YES, wet DCOF >=.42 and Shower Floor YES for a possible walk-in; the point-drain slope/layout and horizontal niche sill assembly are not established by product listings.

confidence: Medium. Official product pages support the colors, formats and surface ratings; the OT04 reference identifies a glossy green shower but not its tile, and the other triplex bath schemes are unavailable in the blind source set.
