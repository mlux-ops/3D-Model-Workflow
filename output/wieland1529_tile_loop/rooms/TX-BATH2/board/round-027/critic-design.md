## Cross-candidate evidence notes

- The room plumbing packet lists the Duravit Starck 59 x 29-1/2 in white built-in tub and Hansgrohe Chrome showerhead. Its notes identify Chrome as consistent with the other shower fittings; some individual fitting rows do not separately repeat the finish. The basin is explicitly unselected. Both candidates correctly preserve the tub, Chrome program, unselected basin and no tiled shower pan.
- ELM maker pages show Copper, Sapphire and Sandstone as Tones 2 x 20 glossy handmade ceramic, 8.5 mm, with Walls and Wet Areas application. Copper is a saturated orange-copper; Sapphire is deep navy; Sandstone is a muted warm beige. Sources: https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/copper-tones ; https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/sapphire-tones ; https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/sandstone-tones (inspected 2026-11-06/07).
- Floor & Decor's Luxe Sand page shows a pale ivory 24 x 24 matte porcelain at $3.49/SF on the inspected page. Riviera Oat is a light beige 24 x 48 matte porcelain with linear grain at $3.99/SF on the inspected page. These are public retail observations, not Chicago-specific reserved pricing. Sources: https://www.flooranddecor.com/porcelain-tile/luxe-sand-matte-porcelain-tile-101317949.html ; https://www.flooranddecor.com/porcelain-tile/riviera-oat-matte-porcelain-tile-101487775.html (inspected 2026-11-06/07).
- geometry.json marks the 38 SF floor, 5.207 ft tub-back wall, 2.60 ft returns and 7.75 ft tiled height as prior drawing-derived/assumed. Openings and niche dimensions are null. No definitive cut layout or niche fit can be claimed from these values.
- Candidate A and B line 15 describe OT01 as showing a dry powder-like bathroom with layered visual contrast. This is unsupported: the inspected OT01 listing for 405 W Armitage Ave #2N exposes one photo showing the exterior building, and its listing copy describes bathroom program only (separate shower, steam shower, double sink and soaking tub). It supplies no bathroom tile, color, layout, grout, niche or metal-finish precedent. Source: https://www.coldwellbankerhomes.com/il/chicago/405-w-armitage-ave-2n/pid_71576670/ ; see research/comps/COMPS.md lines 24–29.
- A is preferred on design balance: the Copper/Sandstone/Luxe Sand palette has a clear focal wall, calm square floor and warm-neutral niche, while retaining a clean contrast with Chrome. B is a strong alternative with better cool-color alignment to Chrome, but its 24 x 48 linear floor introduces greater setting-out and visual-direction uncertainty in this compact room.
