## Basis and arithmetic

The neutral candidates and room-local R027 evidence give the original gross quantities **per copy**, with the original 15% waste already included once: WALL 92.80 SF, NICHE 8.63 SF (provisional pending field measurement), and LEVEL 43.70 SF. Do not add another 15%. The carried material reference is $999.92/copy; $1,249.90/copy is only the candidate's provisional 125% comparison, not an owner-allocated room budget.

Elm Tones Copper/Sapphire wall and Tones Sandstone niche use the same-job 2026-09-04 quote at $6.95/SF and 6.50 SF/carton. This is the product-matched 1529 quote cited in the room-local R027 evidence; it is not transferred to Soho Ivory or any other Elm product. The Floor & Decor public pages were checked 2026-11-06. Luxe Sand (SKU 101317949) displays $3.49/SF and $54.08/box; its specifications state four pieces at 3.88 SF each (15.52 SF/box). Riviera Oat (SKU 101487775) displays $3.99/SF and $63.84/box; its specifications state two pieces at 8 SF each (16 SF/box). Both pages publish wet DCOF >=0.42. Luxe Sand's displayed $/SF and $/box do not reconcile exactly; carton totals below use the displayed $54.08 box price, while the gross-area screen uses the displayed $3.49/SF rate. Riviera's displayed rate and box price reconcile.

The Elm carton costs are calculated at the quoted $6.95/SF over full carton coverage (6.50 SF/carton); actual invoice rounding is unconfirmed. F&D carton costs use the exact displayed box prices. Carton rounding leaves about 11.93 SF surplus per A copy and 13.37 SF per B copy, spread across the three distinct SKUs. These are not full reserve cartons.

For both copies together, the order screen is 30 wall cartons, 4 niche cartons and 6 floor cartons. The resulting tile-only carton totals are **A $1,860.43** and **B $1,918.99**. On the unrounded gross-area screen, the corresponding two-copy totals are **A $1,714.90** and **B $1,758.60**. A is about $29.28/copy ($58.56 across both copies) below B on carton-rounded tile cost.

Compared with the $999.92/copy carried material reference, the carton-rounded tile-only totals are lower by about $69.71/copy for A and $40.43/copy for B. Both are also below the provisional $1,249.90/copy 125% comparison. The R027 candidate explicitly says that the latter is provisional and there is no owner-allocated budget, so this comparison does not establish a budget-gate pass.

The R027 evidence records a $3.99–$4.39/SF public observation range for Riviera Oat. The live page check showed $3.99/SF and $63.84/box at its West Des Moines location. At the recorded $4.39/SF upper observation, a 16-SF carton is $70.24 and B's carton-rounded tile screen would be about $978.70/copy. This sensitivity remains below the $999.92 carried material reference, but still excludes all non-tile costs.
