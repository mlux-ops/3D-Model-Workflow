# PH-BATH3 — Round 011 independent blind market critique

**Seat:** `critic-market`, Old Town listing specialist; **board weight 0.30**; **Penthouse buyer target $2.8M**. These are two neutral, owner-unapproved alternatives. Each is rated independently on **exactly four** distinct 1–5 market criteria, with a **one-decimal arithmetic mean**. Scores are market judgments, **not** technical acceptance or weighted board scoring.

**Independent local benchmark.** The [verified comp register](../../../../research/comps/COMPS.md) contains **14 listings across nine independent building groups** at approximately **$2.4M–$3.2M**. **OT01–OT04** are four sales at **one 405 W Armitage building**, furnishing **ONE** nearby secondary-bath design/marketing precedent: listing text discusses a Jack-and-Jill wet room and decorative hall-bath treatment, **not** any exact bath tile. **OT05** is one other nearby building with primary bath program copy. **LP01–LP03** are one **adjacent Lincoln Park** building advertising natural-stone baths; **GC01/GC02** are geographically secondary and cannot establish Old Town ceramic-tile trends. **No comp bath photos were independently inspected or authenticated**. Neither ceramic color, tile format, grout nor resale premium can be claimed as verified listing differentiation. The project [geometry](../../geometry.json) presumes a **5.00-ft × 2.33-ft tub alcove** with **7.75-ft** tile height, but the [plumbing](../../plumbing.json) carries **Hansgrohe Chrome**, **Delta DT062412 Chrome 4-in square point shower drain** and **an owner-UNSELECTED basin**. **Owner tub versus walk-in scope is unresolved**; the currently open 1527-named model is **not certified as 1529**.
