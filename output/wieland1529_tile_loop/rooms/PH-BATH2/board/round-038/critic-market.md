The horizontal niche sill/installed waterproofing assembly is not established by these tile listings.

confidence: Medium. Product and program facts are attributable; the Old Town photo note does not identify OT04's tile maker, SKU or grout, and broader unit-wide finish coordination is unavailable in the blind source set.
