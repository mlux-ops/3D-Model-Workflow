Therefore strict **exact-measured** floor gate is **FAIL pending verification**; do not claim raw 0.50 or a tested exact point ([Glazzio research](../../../../research/products/glazzio-harmonic-rondo-hnc6615-12x24-dcof-matte.md), [board](../../../../board/BOARD.md)). **Shower-pan DCOF N/A** because built-in tub is retained.

Gross accounting does not prove a non-overlapping takeoff or trim LF. Verify wet tub flange, joint, grout, Matte White-compatible outside edge, finished floor/wood heights, splash/mirror ([A](candidates/A.md), [geometry](../../geometry.json)).

The nominal **~3.24-in return** is *pre-joint/provisional*, neither guaranteed sufficient nor a verified unavoidable under-3-in cut ([plumbing](../../plumbing.json), [geometry](../../geometry.json)).

No owner-approved room budget, HNC/Soho landed quote, current Chicago stock or ETA ⇒ **125% result unknown**, not an invented fail/pass ([A](candidates/A.md), [owner feedback](../../../../state/OWNER_FEEDBACK.md)).
