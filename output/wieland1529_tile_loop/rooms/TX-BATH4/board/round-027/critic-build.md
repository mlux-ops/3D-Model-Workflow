# TX-BATH4 — R027 BUILD critique

Scope: independent BUILD review of neutral candidates A and B using the TX-BATH4 room geometry, plumbing, the room-local R027 blind product evidence, and the linked product pages. Owner feedback confirms 1527 and 1529 Wieland are the same building; the room geometry values remain drawing-derived or assumed, and no live Revit element IDs or measured dimensions are supplied in this room packet. No native Revit measurements are inferred here.

## Common room conditions

- Two physical copies; prior drawing-derived floor area 38.5 SF per copy. The assumed tub alcove is 5.00 ft wide by 2.54 ft deep. The tub back wall W1 is 5.00 ft; returns W2/W3 are 2.54 ft each; tiled height is 7.75 ft and ceiling height 9.25 ft.
- Openings, sill elevations, actual niche aperture/location, wall plumbness, substrate build-up, floor substrate/flatness, and tub rough/flange dimensions are unknown. These are material limits on a construction-ready cut and setting-out review.
- Plumbing selects a Duravit Starck 59 x 29-1/2 in built-in tub and Hansgrohe Matte White tub/shower trim. This is a tub surround: no tiled shower pan and no slope-to-drain criterion.
- Both candidates use the same ELM Cortar Charcoal FLAT 24x48 wall tile and a 2x20 ELM Tones niche tile. ELM's Cortar page lists 24x48, Walls and Wet Areas; the room-local R027 evidence records 9 mm thickness. The live page does not expose actual dimensions or thickness, and its linked catalog PDF did not load. The Tones page fetches were unavailable; niche format/application rely on the room-local evidence.

## Gate flags, top fix and confidence

- **Geometry gate — pending for both; no failure demonstrated.** The supplied drawing labels do not prove an under-3 in cut, but the nominal 3.24 in centered return cuts are close to the threshold. Actual tile dimensions, grout, trim, openings, niche, floor perimeter and tub interface must be field-verified before clearing the gate.
- **Slope-to-drain — N/A for both.** Duravit tub selected; there is no tiled shower pan.
- **Top fix for both:** produce one field-verified setting-out plan showing W1/W2/W3, floor perimeter and threshold, tub rough/flange/rim, niche opening and edge profiles; set centerlines and tile orientation, keep any running bond within 1/3 offset, and demonstrate at least 3 in at every visible cut before release.
- **Confidence:** medium-low. The main dimensions are drawing-derived/assumed, actual openings and substrate are absent, and the ELM maker page does not expose the exact tile dimensions/thickness in its live text.
