# TX-BATH4 — R027 DESIGN critique

**Seat:** Interior designer (.25)  
**Review:** Blind comparison of neutral candidates A and B. No private mapping or other-room records were consulted.

## Shared room and fixture conditions

Both candidates retain the listed white Duravit Starck 59 x 29-1/2 in. built-in tub and Hansgrohe Matte White trim. The charcoal wet-wall field gives the white tub and white controls a clear, deliberate contrast; there is no high-severity finish or form clash. The Ecostat S thermostat / volume-control diverter and tub-spout / rough compatibility hold remains separate and unresolved. No fixture change is indicated by this tile review. This is a tub alcove: **no tiled shower pan and no shower curb**.

The geometry basis is provisional: 38.5 SF floor; W1 back wall 5.00 ft; W2/W3 returns 2.54 ft each; tiled height 7.75 ft. These values are drawing-derived or assumed, not live-Revit verified. Actual openings, niche dimensions and tile cuts remain unknown. The room-only packet contains no other triplex-bath finish selections, so same-unit coordination receives a neutral score pending the prime's cross-room synthesis. OT01 at 405 W Armitage is described in the packet as a dry powder-like bathroom; it supports layered contrast only, not a wet-bath tile or plumbing comparison.
