# Round 013 — independent interior DESIGN critique — PH-PRIMARY

**Blind scope:** Current neutral candidates **A** and **B** only. For each letter, the five distinct interior-design criteria are scored **1–5**, then averaged arithmetically to **one decimal**. This independent DESIGN seat carries a board weight of 0.25, applied exclusively by the prime; no weighted total, candidate-history identity or selection result is inferred here.

**Controlling room evidence:** The [selected PH Primary plumbing packet](../../plumbing.json) specifies Brizo **BT0734816-BNX Black Onyx 48-in LINEAR reversible drain** plus **BR73480 rough**, matching Roccesco controls, and **separate Duravit 700337000000090 soaking tub**. There is **NO Primary niche**. The [historical geometry](../../geometry.json) is **103 SF**, **111-in wet-wall height**, nominal shower walls **W1 70.248 in / W2 55.752 in**, and a shared WC included within the original Primary floor pool; no 1529-native as-built dimensions or locations are verified. The [PH owner price band](../../../../state/OWNER_FEEDBACK.md) is **$2.8M**, with no approved material cap. The [comps](../../../../research/comps/COMPS.md) comprise 14 records from nine distinct buildings, with **OT01–OT04 one 405 W Armitage precedent**. Their sales descriptions support sophisticated primary bath *programs*, never a validated Bone, relief, grout or floor finish precedent. Cohesion with the current PH Bath2 and Bath4 proposals is an **architectural judgment** using their authorized [Bath2 B](../../../PH-BATH2/board/round-013/candidates/B.md) and [Bath4 A](../../../PH-BATH4/board/round-013/candidates/A.md) packets; the other PH rooms' final finishes are unconfirmed.

**Arithmetic mean:** (4 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 1) / 5 = **3.0 / 5.0**.

**gate_flags:**
- **WET_WALL_REED_FAIL:** [ELM Bone Reed Relief 24x48](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/bone-reed-cortar) lacks individual **Wet Areas** listing for the specified shower/wet ceiling; [flat Bone](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/cortar-bone) passes wall use only and cannot transfer its rating.
- **LEVEL_SOFT_RAW_DCOF_FAIL:** Bone Soft 24x48 bath floor **and shared WC** exact SKU/finish wet numerical measured value, lab record and method are absent ([A](candidates/A.md), [R05 audit](../../../../research/products/R05_WET_LISTINGS_DCOF_INSTALLATION_EVIDENCE.md)).
- **LINEAR_PAN_ANTISLIP_RAW_DCOF_FAIL:** Bone Anti-Slip 12x24 **separate linear shower PAN** exact wet measured numeric result missing; finish wording and visual texture cannot substitute for testing or proved linear slope ([A](candidates/A.md)).
- **LINEAR_SYSTEM_AND_MODEL_HOLD:** Selected Brizo **BT0734816-BNX/BR73480**, waterproof connection, drain position, pitch, boundary to WC, ceiling attachment and 1529-certified cuts/areas are not verified ([plumbing](../../plumbing.json), [geometry](../../geometry.json)).
- **SCOPE_COST_HOLD:** Historical **143.0-SF wall/ceiling** and **135.6-SF pooled LEVEL+shared WC+PAN** gross each already include one **15% waste**, no extra WC/pan pool or niche, and room cap/installed quote/owner release are null ([A](candidates/A.md), [owner feedback](../../../../state/OWNER_FEEDBACK.md)). No evidenced severe metal-finish clash or calculable 125%-budget failure.

**confidence:** **medium** in finish and maker wall-use distinctions; **low** in verified lighting, cuts, ceiling/waterproof engineering and floor test readiness.

**gate_flags:**
- **FLAT_BONE_WET_WALL_PASS:** [ELM Cortar Bone flat Soft](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/cortar-bone) lists Wet Areas for shower walls; wet ceiling attachment still requires an engineered substrate/retention detail.

- **LINEAR_PAN_RL21_HEX_RAW_DCOF_FAIL:** RL21 **1.5-in hex Matte** is a distinct mosaic SKU, **no exact measured PAN wet DCOF**; historical Prime linear-mosaic target **≥0.60** versus later **≥0.42** are both non-measured application criteria ([version evidence](../../../../research/products/R07_DALTILE_PRIME_LINEAR_DRAIN_ALTERNATIVE_VERSIONED_EVIDENCE.md)).
- **BRIZO_RL21_HEIGHT_MEMBRANE_HOLD:** [Brizo](https://www.brizo.com/bath/product/BT0734816-BNX) has a solid reversible grate (rough **BR73480** separately required) and optional **7–18-mm tile-in frame**. [R13 product audit](../../../../research/products/R13_PH_RL21_MULTI_FORMAT_LEVEL_LINEAR_POINT_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md) identifies **1/4-in brochure** versus **5/16-in individual live hex-page** discrepancy. The **solid grate avoids only the tile-in decorative insert thickness question**; does not approve actual hex-plus-mortar thickness, grate edge, drain throat, bonded membrane, shower slope or flood testing.
- **1529_AND_OWNER_RELEASE_HOLD:** Existing model titled **1527**, true 1529 layout/curb/ceiling not measured. Original **143.0-SF wet wall/ceiling** and **135.6-SF LEVEL+WC+PAN** gross include 15% waste once; no niche, no extra shower or WC whole-area order. Signed owner room cap, current bid/lot, sample/approval absent ([B](candidates/B.md), [geometry](../../geometry.json)).

**confidence:** **medium** for flat Bone/Black Onyx color and exact manufacturer format distinction; **low** for numeric floor tests, installed grout/grate elevations, membrane and site measurements.

**Independent design finding:** B offers a more coherent, technically bounded linear-floor composition than A, but remains unapproved pending distinct raw LEVEL/PAN wet values, the actual 1529 survey and an agreed Brizo drain/waterproof assembly. These are design-seat judgments only.
