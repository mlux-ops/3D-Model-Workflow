|---|---:|---|
| Old Town comparable visual positioning | **3.2** | OT04's actual wet-wall photograph conveys gloss, color and visual hierarchy; uniform matte concrete gray across wall/floor/pan offers a different, materially quieter signal and is not proven to match that premium photographed character. |

**Arithmetic:** (3.2 + 3.4 + 4.0 + 3.6) / 4 = **3.55** → **overall MARKET seat score 3.6/5.0**.

**top_fix:** Change the dominant wet-wall appearance to a warmer, wet-qualified mineral finish while preserving the large-format floor/LINEAR PAN concept and BNX fixture selection.

**gate_flags:** **No established selected-product hard-gate failure.** Exact [Perimeter Gray 101239218](https://www.flooranddecor.com/porcelain-tile/perimeter-gray-matte-porcelain-tile-101239218.html) lists Shower Walls YES, Shower Floor YES, Bathroom Floor YES and wet DCOF ≥0.42; its **Kirkwood** public page is **$3.99/SF** with no current local-discontinued flag, but this proves neither Chicago lot nor sustained supply. **OPEN:** overhead shower ceiling tile independent HOLD; actual calibrated 24×48 dimension, field cuts, BNX linear-pan setout, tub/wall transitions, Chicago delivered cost and owner allowance. No owner veto identified. Historical screened $1,111.61/copy is material-rate only ([B candidate](candidates/B.md)).

**confidence: medium.** Comp photograph supports differentiated shower character, not a statistically measured preference for colored vs gray primary bathrooms.

**Seat-only observation:** A has stronger premium warmth and broad primary-bath appeal; B has lower material-rate exposure but needs visual depth. **No cross-seat weighting or champion declaration.**
