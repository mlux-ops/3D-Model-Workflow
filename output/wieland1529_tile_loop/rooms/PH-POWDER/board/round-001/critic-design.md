# PH-POWDER — blind Round 001, critic-design

Seat: interior designer; board weight **0.25**. Five criteria rated **1–5**; overall seat score is the arithmetic mean, rounded to one decimal. This is a **dry vanity feature wall**, not a shower/tub wet wall: the **28-SF** room retains **wood floor** with **no tile-floor scope** ([geometry](../../geometry.json)). The carried faucet is Brizo Frank Lloyd Wright wall mount **T65722LF-GL, Luxe Gold** and matching drain **RP72414GL**; the AXOR Brushed Gold Optic option mentioned in the fixture deck has not superseded the carried selection ([plumbing](../../plumbing.json)). The drawing-derived feature wall is **4.224 LF × 9 ft 3 in.** with unverified openings and wall-mount rough-in coordinates.

## Candidate A — Vera Forma Earth Lines, glossy relief

| Design criterion | Rating | Evidence-based reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Plumbing finish and form | **4/5** | Warm **Earth Lines** relief can complement **Brizo Luxe Gold** and the Frank Lloyd Wright trim's architectural vocabulary, but the glossy relief could compete with the wall-mount faucet backplate unless the two are mocked up together ([A packet](candidates/A.md); [plumbing](../../plumbing.json); [Vera Forma](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product-collection/vera-forma)). |
| Cohesion across the PH bathrooms | **4/5** | An earth-toned wall relates to Bath 3's Sandstone/Bone and Bath 4's Bone palette; full-height dimensional ornament also gives the powder its own focal character ([A packet](candidates/A.md); PH-BATH3 [A](../../../../rooms/PH-BATH3/board/round-001/candidates/A.md); PH-BATH4 [B](../../../../rooms/PH-BATH4/board/round-001/candidates/B.md)). |
| Light, perceived size and scale | **4/5** | **2 × 10-in.** glossy lines can bring reflection and texture to a **4.224-ft** vanity wall, but at **9 ft 3 in.** high, light rake and sustained relief intensity need physical evaluation ([A packet](candidates/A.md); [geometry](../../geometry.json)). |
| Grout, alignment and layout | **3/5** | The proposal has no locked stack/offset, vertical-versus-horizontal rib direction, sample grout or cuts at faucet penetrations and ceiling ([A packet](candidates/A.md); [geometry](../../geometry.json)). |
| Niche, edges and transitions | **3/5** | The feature is dry and has no niche; a single tiled focal wall against existing wood floor is clear, but exposed returns, wall-end trim, vanity/mirror edge and backplate flatness remain uncoordinated ([A packet](candidates/A.md); [geometry](../../geometry.json); [plumbing](../../plumbing.json)). |

**Overall seat score: 3.6/5.0** (18/25).

**top_fix:** Make a full-scale faucet/backplate-and-tile-relief mock-up to select joint direction and a flat mounting detail before committing to the full-height wall.

**gate_flags: none.** Manufacturer lists **Vera Forma** for **Walls** and the specified tile occupies a dry feature wall only; no shower Wet Areas or floor DCOF gate applies ([Vera Forma](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product-collection/vera-forma); [A packet](candidates/A.md); [geometry](../../geometry.json)). **No HIGH plumbing finish/form clash** is established against carried Luxe Gold; the concern is competing relief at the faucet, still untested.

**confidence: medium.** Fixture finish and tile purpose are documented; relief pattern appearance, lighting, faucet projection and actual terminations are not verified by a sample/elevation.

**gate_flags: none.** Manufacturer lists **Haze 1.6 × 5 Matte** for **Walls**; proposed surface is a **dry** vanity wall, with wood floor remaining, so Wet Areas and wet-floor DCOF gates do not apply ([Haze manufacturer](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product-collection/haze); [C packet](candidates/C.md); [geometry](../../geometry.json)). **No HIGH plumbing finish/form clash** is supported; the image-to-sample color uncertainty is a design verification item.

**confidence: medium.** Manufacturer confirms size/finish/application and carried fixture finish is explicit; physical mix, true shade, grout and verified wall-mount faucet/elevation are outstanding.
