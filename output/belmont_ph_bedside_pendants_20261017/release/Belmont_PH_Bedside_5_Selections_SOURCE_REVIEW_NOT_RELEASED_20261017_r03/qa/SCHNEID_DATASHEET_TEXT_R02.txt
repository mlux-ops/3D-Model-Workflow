the STUCCO LAMP
Embodying a harmonious duality of clear forms and softened surfaces, the Stucco lamp 
stands for a poetic union of sensibility and dynamic design. The thoughtful interplay of 
smooth and tactile ceramic surfaces imbues depth and character, creating a captivating 
sensory experience. The Stucco lamp stands out with its paired sculptural pieces  — a 
meticulously crafted canopy for the ceiling and a socket designed to embrace the warm 
glow of a globe bulb. This synergy of elements not only bathes your space in light but 
also unveils an artful approach to bringing creative luminosity into any environment.
designed by Julia Jessen | 2024
