# Source selection — ELM Colorado Bruges Antique, Exa mosaic

The [ELM Bruges Antique listing](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/colorado-bruges-antique) names a Mosaic Exa 12 x 12 option, but does not publish a flat image of that exact finish and mosaic combination. Its 12 x 12 size is a nominal listing. The [Kronos Bruges Exa SKU 8521 page](https://kronosceramiche.it/prodotto/bruges-exa/) gives a 25.5 x 29.5 cm (10 x 11 5/8 in) sheet, 9 mm thickness, natural surface, and V2 color variation. The manufacturer image is landscape, so this material uses 29.5 cm horizontally and 25.5 cm vertically per photographed sheet.

| Source | Use | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| [Kronos Bruges Antique collection](https://kronosceramiche.com/collections/carriere-du-kronos/) — `grid_bruges_antique.jpg` | Accepted for color and stone-face detail | Flat, straight-on 4 x 4 array of 16 distinct antique stone faces. Interiors are cropped clear of baked joints. |
| Same collection — `antique-bruges_1-600x600.jpg` | Color cross-check only | Flat swatch, but a single small circular display lacks the variation of the 16-face grid. |
| [Kronos Bruges Exa SKU 8521](https://kronosceramiche.it/prodotto/bruges-exa/) — `8521-3.jpg` | Accepted for hex geometry and ratio of plain, worn and diagonally grooved faces | Straight-on mosaic sample with visible joints. Its color is the standard Bruges image, not independently confirmed as Antique. |

The delivered maps combine the Antique finish photo with the Exa pattern. The exact printed distribution of an Antique Exa sheet is not documented by these public images. Grout width, grout depth and groove depth are visual estimates. No room-scene lighting or perspective image is copied into the color map.
