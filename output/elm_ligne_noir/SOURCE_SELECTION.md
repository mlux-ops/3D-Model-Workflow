## Measured facts and conflicts

- Flutes: 10 per tile face, reeded profile (rounded convex rib ≈ 55 % of each 0.294 in unit, flat channel ≈ 45 %). An early automatic count of 37.8 "ridges" in the swatch was the 4th harmonic of the flute profile; epoch folding gives the true 36.03 px period.
- Joint: 1/16 in (0.21 flute widths in the wall photo). Grout: light grey, sRGB ≈ 220/214/216 as photographed.
- **Conflict, disclosed:** both independent wall photos give a module length/width ratio of 3.48 and 3.50, not the 3.33 of a 3 × 10 module (real size may be metric, e.g. about 7 × 25 cm). The maps follow the stated 3 × 10 nominal as the installed module (repo rule); confirm against a physical sample if exact tile count matters.
- Single swatch: every tile face is built from the same ~9 real flute strips (re-sequenced, length-flipped, tone-varied). Genuine face-to-face variety is therefore limited; a second straight-on swatch photo would raise it.
