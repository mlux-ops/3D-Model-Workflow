# QuietMount: independent edge clips

Three separate screw-mounted clips per sample. No frame, strip, or mounting pad behind the sample. Two identical lower clips carry its weight; the upper clip is inverted and slides upward on a screw slot for removal. The wall remains visible through the entire sample except the three small front lips.

## Files and quantities

Open QuietMount_Independent_H2D.3mf in Bambu Studio. This is an unsliced native project with 15 clips arranged on one plate, H2D 0.4 mm, Textured PEI, PolyTerra PLA, 0.08 mm layers, six walls and solid infill. Slice before printing. Each column contains two lower clips and one upper clip. Columns left to right: 12.7, 19.05, 19.05, 20, 22.225 mm. Individual STL files use millimeters and are already oriented on their broad sides. Do not rotate them upright: side printing keeps the arm and base continuous within each layer. No supports are configured; the small transverse screw bores may need minor cleanup.

## Hardware to buy

15 pan-head screws, approximately 3.5 mm shaft diameter (#6 nominal), with heads 6.5–8 mm across. The printed holes/slots are 4.4 mm wide. Use matching drywall anchors intended for your wall thickness and screw size; follow the anchor manufacturer's specified screw length and installation instructions, allowing for the 4 mm printed base. Do not substitute countersunk/flat-head screws: these clips have flat bearing faces. Screw heads remain accessible outside the sample outline.

## Installation

1. Test one set with its actual sample before installing all five. Remove rough edges from contact surfaces. The depth allows a nominal 1.5 mm wall gap plus 0.4 mm clearance; there is no rear spacer. The sample may settle closer to the wall.
2. Mark a level line for the sample's bottom edge. Place lower clip centers 56 mm apart (28 mm either side of center). Their screw centers are 14 mm BELOW that line. Mount both lower clips with their ledges level and lips pointing upward. Tighten by hand only enough to stop rotation; do not crush the plastic.
3. Place the sample on the ledges. Center the inverted upper clip above it, leaving about 0.6 mm between its underside and the sample's top edge. All of its wall base must be ABOVE the sample. Mark the slot's end nearest the sample: this is the locked position and leaves 6 mm of upward travel. For an exact 95.25 mm square, this screw center is about 106.85 mm above the lower ledge line. Use the actual sample to mark it, not that nominal dimension alone.
4. Remove the sample before drilling/installing the upper anchor. Fit the top clip loosely. Slide it up, install the sample, slide it down to overlap the front edge, then snug the screw. Do not clamp tightly against brittle material. To remove the sample, support it, loosen the upper screw and slide that clip upward.

Bases extend 22 mm beyond the top/bottom edges and are 12 mm wide. Front lips overlap the sample by 3 mm and are 12 mm wide, with 2.4 mm thickness. They are wider than the earlier tiny tabs to give these independent cantilevers more material. There is no material touching or spanning the rear face. Small edge-contact pads can be added if needed, but their thickness must be allowed for in the fit.

## Limits

This is an untested printable prototype, not a load-rated mounting product. Mesh closure and positive volume were checked; physical fit, long-term PLA creep, anchor performance and retention have not been tested. The terrazzo estimate from your dimensions/weight specification is about 443 g. Start with that sample's 20 mm set in a low, protected location and check for movement before mounting higher. Keep PLA away from heat and direct sun. The project is configured but not sliced or physically printed.
