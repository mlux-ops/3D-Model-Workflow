# Ten sets — snug covers only

Open **QuietMount_Covers_Only_READY.3mf** as a project in Bambu Studio. One plate contains **10 lower snug covers and 10 upper snug covers**. There are no screw-mounted holders or fit coupons on this plate.

The covers use the exact snug STL geometry from the successful physical test. Both sizes retain the fingernail removal groove. They print face-down, with the retaining petals upward, on Textured PEI. The preset is for H2D 0.4 mm Standard nozzles and PolyTerra PLA: 0.08 mm layers, six walls, solid rectilinear infill, no supports or brims. Select your loaded PolyTerra spool when sending the plate.

Bambu estimate: **2 hours 7 minutes, 9.5 g**. Native slicing completed without warnings, and the final project was inspected to confirm that all 20 objects are snug covers.

There are five columns. The first two rows are five lower/upper cover pairs; the next two rows are another five pairs. All lower covers are identical, and all upper covers are identical, regardless of sample thickness.

## Pending holders

The screw-mounted bodies are on hold while the actual screws are measured. Planned sample thicknesses, in set order A–J: **12.7, 19.05, 19.05, 20, 22.225, 20.5, 18.5, 18, 14, 17 mm**.

These covers fit the existing nominal 8 mm recess. The screw measurements will determine whether that recess can be retained. A change to its diameter could require revised covers; this covers-only plate intentionally preserves the fit the user has already tested.

For the next revision, record the screw's maximum head diameter, head height, shaft diameter, length under the head, and whether its underside is flat or tapered.
