# QuietMount — rounded tabs, tab-down printing

Open QuietMount_Tab_Down_READY.3mf in Bambu Studio. Plate 1 contains all 20 bodies already tab-face down; plate 2 contains 20 matching cosmetic covers. No manual rotation needed.

Nine tapered lower holders (A–I), one mushroom lower holder (J), and ten mushroom upper keepers. Set thicknesses in mm: A 12.7; B 19.05; C 19.05; D 20; E 22.225; F 20.5; G 18.5; H 18; I 14; J 17.

Lower tabs have a circular crown with tangent curved shoulder transitions. Upper tabs have a continuous rounded outline joining the keeper sides. Previous side-print end flats removed. Vertical screw holes are circular instead of teardrop-shaped. Sample clearances, tab projection, mixed screw seats, and nominal snug cover retention retained. Print the included covers for the matching untrimmed exterior outline.

H2D 0.4 mm, Textured PEI, PolyTerra PLA, 0.08 mm layers, six walls and 100% rectilinear infill. Bodies have 3 mm external brims. Covers print face down without brims. No support material is specified; the internal mushroom seat requires a short annular bridge above the head recess. Peel brims carefully to preserve the plate-facing tab outline.

This orientation puts PEI texture on the front-facing tab surface and aligns the rounded side outlines with XY motion. It also changes the layer direction through the tab root; improved appearance is not evidence of equal mechanical strength. The new orientation, fit and strength have not been physically tested. Separate mushroom screws' compatibility with the anchors remains unverified.

Native slicing is performed to create the printable project. Extended final geometry checks skipped per prior user request. See slice_summary.json for final estimates and slicer warnings.
