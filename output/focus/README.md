# DCW Focus X3 Black - Revit 2025

Face-based Lighting Fixtures visualization family. Place on the underside of a native ceiling/floor face. Main file: DCW_Focus_X3_Black_Adjustable_R2025.rfa. The mobile is nested inside; it does not require a separate load.

Cord Length is an instance parameter measured from canopy underside to the top of the mobile. Total Drop is calculated as Cord Length + 1063 mm. Default total drop is 60 inches. Keep cord length within the documented 3 m maximum; manufacturer recommends at least 200 mm clearance where drafts or ventilation occur.

The mobile keeps fixed dimensions while the main suspension changes. Black metal and softly luminous translucent acrylic have editable material type parameters. The glow is appearance only, without IES or electrical connectors.

Sources: manufacturer's Focus X3 US technical sheet and installation sheet, linked from https://dcw-editions.com/fr-us/products/focus-3-etl . Canopy diameter 230 mm and thickness 26 mm; mobile height 1037 mm; two 364 mm discs and one 284 mm disc. Bar lengths, plan angles, fine frame sections, cable diameters, and disc thickness are inferred from manufacturer drawings and photos. Mobile width varies with its composition; the source calls it indicative. The assembly is a fixed representative arrangement, not a kinetic simulation.
