DESCRIPTION 

Summit's collection of premium wine cellars brings full functionality, convenience, and elevated style to kitchens and home bars with unique space limitations.

The SDHWC98PNR is a 24" wide dual zone wine cellar that features a front-breathing design for built-in installation. Sized at just 18" deep, this wine cellar offers slim-fitting elegance, perfect for narrow home bars and kitchen corners, converted spaces,  or other spots where you can't fit a full-depth unit. The reversible double pane tempered glass door features a fully articulating European-style hinge design to ensure a smoother opening. We include a 2-piece bracket kit that allows easy installation of your own custom door trim to match or complement adjacent cabinetry. This unit has a sealed rear and right-angle cord for a better fit. A lock is located in the kickplate for added convenience and security.

Inside, model SDHWC98PNR includes a stainless steel rear wall for improved durability and sanitation. Tri-colored LED lighting featuring amber, blue, and white options offers elegant display conditions with dimmable levels that let you create the perfect presentation. The easy-to-use digital control panel allows you to manage the upper temperature chamber between 41 and 50ºF for white or sparkling wine, while the lower zone is designed to store red wine between 50 and 64ºF. Fan-forced cooling ensures even temperature distribution and fast temperature recovery. This unit comes with 15 shelves in a black wire finish with stainless steel front trim. The shelves are full-extension and set on ball-bearing glides for smooth loading.  Optimal storage capacity accommodates up to 98 standard 750ml Bordeaux or Burgundy style bottles (note: your capacity may vary depending on the bottle style).

Model SDHWC98PNR has two different running modes: Silent mode, which minimizes vibrations to lower noise emissions and creates a more stable storage environment, ideal for preserving your wine's quality. The second, DynaClima mode, optimizes both temperature and humidity distribution for long-term wine storage, mimicking the conditions of a traditional wine storage cabinet. 

Additional features include a temperature memory function to restore set temperature in the case of power loss and Sabbath mode, which disables certain functions during periods of religious observance. A built-in alarm audibly warns if the door is left ajar or if the unit rises or falls out of the recommended temperature range. 

With its uniquely shallow depth, elegant design, and premium features, the SDHWC98PNR brings true luxury to the home. Explore Summit's extensive catalog of wine cellars and beverage centers at summitappliance.com

NOTE: Due to its unique size, this unit should not be used freestanding. To prevent tipping, install the anti-tip bracket included in your packaging when setting up the product.
