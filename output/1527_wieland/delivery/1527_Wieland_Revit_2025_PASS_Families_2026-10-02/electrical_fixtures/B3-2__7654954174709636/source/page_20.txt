Rona 24-in Vanity Light
SKU:
VL17024-BG
Finish
Brushed Gold
Black
Brushed Nickel
Chrome
A tubular length of illuminated rod the Rona wall light is supported and clasped with a detailed cylindrical projection. The thick square outline of the attachment plate frames the lamp holder and echoes the refinement of present architectural trim. Available as either a sconce or vanity and in several curated finishes.
Integrated LED with 3000K Warm White Color Temperature
Designed for bathroom, entryway, powder room, hallway, and beyond
Modern, Contemporary Style with Decorative Details
Installation hardware included
Easy assembly instructions included in box
Dimmable, see specs for detail
