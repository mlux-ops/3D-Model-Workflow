Oyster Wall Sconce
SPEC #:
HNM1353351

The Oyster Wall Sconce features a frosted acrylic diffuser nestled into its oblong metal frame like a pearl within a shell. The integrated LED light source casts a soft glow within the frame. Available with Frosted smooth or Patterned, frosted acrylic diffuser. Dimmable with TRIAC, ELV, or 0-10V dimmers.
Available Options
Finish: Black, Brushed Brass, Brushed Copper, Brushed Stainless Steel, White
