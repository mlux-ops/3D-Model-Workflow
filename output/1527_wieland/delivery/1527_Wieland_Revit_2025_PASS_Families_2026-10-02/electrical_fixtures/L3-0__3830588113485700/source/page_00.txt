Sky Dome Pendant

SKY DOM 18 BLK- 3000K
The Sky Dome Pendant is a clean, detailed piece that will bring a bold presence into a variety of interior spaces. A dome in Wood or Spun Aluminum creates an eye catching element. Provides both up and down light for complete illumination.
Note:
Walnut and Oak domes are not available in 32 inch diameter.
