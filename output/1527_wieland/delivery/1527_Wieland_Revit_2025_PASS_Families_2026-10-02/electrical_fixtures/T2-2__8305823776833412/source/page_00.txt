Exhaust Pendant
SPEC #:
BPU1342906
ID:
NEIG-943939
The Exhaust Pendant features the Buster + Punch signature linear or cross pattern knurl baffle that captures light and creates a delicate metallic glow. An included honeycomb filter emits a precise, non-glare, directional spotlight. Available with a Graphite or Stone finish and matching flush canopy complemented by a variety of baffle finishes.
Note:
Not all finish combinations are available.
Available Options
Finish: Graphite, Stone
Color: Black, Brass, Burnt Steel, Gun Metal, Smoked Bronze, Steel
