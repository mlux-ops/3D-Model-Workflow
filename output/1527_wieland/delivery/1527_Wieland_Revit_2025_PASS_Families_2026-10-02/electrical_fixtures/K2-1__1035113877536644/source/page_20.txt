Vista 9-in Exterior Wall Sconce
Vista 9-in Exterior Wall Sconce
SKU:
EW22109-BK
Finish
Black
The distinctly minimal Vista sconce is composed of perpendicular proportional planes. Open at both ends the extruded rectangle emanates a crisp V-shaped light pattern up and down. The excellent craftsmanship is prominently displayed with the simplicity of form. The curated selection of fine finishes allow the fixture to accent a space with color and texture or blend into the environment.
Integrated LED with 3000K Warm White Color Temperature
Designed for any indoor or outdoor usage
Modern, Contemporary Style with Decorative Details
Installation hardware included
Easy assembly instructions included in box
Dimmable, see specs for detail
