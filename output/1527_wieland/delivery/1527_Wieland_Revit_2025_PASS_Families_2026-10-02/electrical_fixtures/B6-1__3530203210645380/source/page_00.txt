Designed by Sean Lavin
Read Bio
Silas Wall Light by Sean Lavin features clean lines and precise detailing that define its minimalist character. Clear acrylic cylindrical diffusers pair with integrated LED light sources to create a crisp, modern effect. Each diffuser sits against a streamlined frame to emphasize balance and proportion without excess. With its elongated profile, the sconce is ideal for living rooms, dining rooms, larger bathrooms, and walk-in closets. The Silas Collection brings refinement to all contemporary interiors, offering lighting that is both sculptural and functional.

About Sean Lavin
Known for his sophisticated, modern designs combined with the latest advancements in lighting technology and expert craftsmanship, multiple lighting design award winner Sean Lavin finds inspiration in experiencing new environments, cultures, architecture and fashion. He believes lighting is just as powerful as other major design elements to create a specific style and mood in a space.
Sean Lavin is an award-winning designer with a background in Graphic Design and Fine Art. Inspired by his travels around the world, Lavin believes that lighting is one of the most important design elements in a room. He works with Generation Lighting to create collections that combine advanced technology with expert craftsmanship to produce simply stunning pieces. He is currently the Chief Design Officer for Visual Comfort & Co.
