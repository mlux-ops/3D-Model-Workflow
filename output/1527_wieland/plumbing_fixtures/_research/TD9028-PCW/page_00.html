Description

This drain plug trim kit from Westbrass provides an easy to use, European style tip-toe cover and an Illusionary overflow cover. Perfect for the sleek even lines in today’s designs, this bath tub waste and overflow trim cleanly covers overflow and outlet, and is ADA approved for easy closure. This unit fits coarse thread drains and two-hole overflow elbows.
