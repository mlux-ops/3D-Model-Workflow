Power .75HP with EZ Connect Cord & Hardwire Adapter

SHARE

The InSinkErator Power .75HP 3/4 HP Garbage Disposal offers a clean, hygienic, convenient and environmentally responsible way to rid your kitchen of food scraps. Septic-safe for properly maintained septic systems, this compact garbage disposal quickly grinds food waste into tiny particles that easily flush down the kitchen drain. Part of the InSinkErator Power Series, InSinkErator garbage disposals designed to help prevent clogs by grinding food scraps finer*, the Power .75HP disposal features a 3/4 HP, heavy-duty Dura-Drive induction motor with more jam-busting torque to power through tough food scraps like peach pits. The rust-resistant, stainless steel grind components offer lasting durability, and the compact design fits easily under the kitchen sink, leaving room for other kitchen necessities. In addition to reduced sound, this EZ Connect InSinkErator garbage disposal features the EZ Connect Cord & Hardwire Adapter, EZ Removable Dishwasher Inlet Plug, and Lift and Latch technology for easy installation. Features

Download Case Study
  • INSINKERATOR POWER .75HP 3/4 HP GARBAGE DISPOSAL: 12-inch T x 8.25-inch W disposal with EZ Connect Power Cord & Hardwire Adapter, garbage disposal flange, gaskets, etc., for install, plus wrenchette and kitchen drain stopper
  • DESIGNED TO HELP PREVENT CLOGS: Sink disposal with 2-Stage MULTIGRIND Technology designed to help prevent clogs by grinding food scraps finer; 3/4 HP Dura-Drive induction motor for maximum durability; removable baffle for easy cleaning
  • DIY EZ CONNECT SINK GARBAGE DISPOSAL: USA-assembled kitchen appliance with EZ Connect Power Cord & Hardwire Adapter, EZ Removable Dishwasher Inlet Plug, and Lift and Latch technology for the easiest installation of an InSinkErator food waste disposer
  • REDUCED SOUND AND COMPACT: Garbage disposer that operates with reduced noise levels compared to standard disposals; space-saving design that easily fits under sink, leaving room for other small kitchen appliances, like an instant hot water dispenser
  • 6-YEAR LIMITED SERVICE WARRANTY: Backed by InSinkErator 6-Year "We Come to You" Limited Service Warranty that covers in-home repair or replacement of your kitchen sink garbage disposal by an authorized dealer
  • EZ Connect Disposal easily connects to a hardwire connection or outlet.
  • Removable, Diswasher Safe, Quiet Collar® Sink Baffle
  • Safe for use with properly maintained septic tanks.
