Designed by Sean Lavin
Read Bio
The Pylon Pendant displays modern elegance with its long tapered metal body punctuated at the tip with a brilliant clear acrylic optic. Beautiful as a standalone or clustered in multiples, this pendant adds a dash of sophistication to living rooms, lounges, and dining rooms. A 120-277V universal driver is included, dimmable with a TRIAC, ELV, or 0-10V dimmer.
The Natural Brass is a living finish that will patina, warming over time. If it becomes uneven or spotty, a brass cleaner can restore the finish.
The Pylon series is part of the Trellis collection which represents modern, minimalist lighting in its purest form.
Two canopy options are available:
4.6 Inch Diameter:
4.6 inch diameter canopy. Driver to be installed in a standard junction box.
2.6 Inch Diameter:
2.6 inch diameter canopy that offers a minimal look and mounts to an included mini junction box. Driver to be installed in a remote accessible location.

About Sean Lavin
Known for his sophisticated, modern designs combined with the latest advancements in lighting technology and expert craftsmanship, multiple lighting design award winner Sean Lavin finds inspiration in experiencing new environments, cultures, architecture and fashion. He believes lighting is just as powerful as other major design elements to create a specific style and mood in a space.
Sean Lavin is an award-winning designer with a background in Graphic Design and Fine Art. Inspired by his travels around the world, Lavin believes that lighting is one of the most important design elements in a room. He works with Generation Lighting to create collections that combine advanced technology with expert craftsmanship to produce simply stunning pieces. He is currently the Chief Design Officer for Visual Comfort & Co.
