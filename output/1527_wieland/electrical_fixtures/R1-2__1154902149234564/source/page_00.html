    Zephyr Smart 3-Blade Fan with Color-Select Light

    SPEC #: MFR918914

    ID: FR-W2006-52L-MB

    The Zephyr Ceiling fan is a luxurious and stylish three blade fan featuring a robust LED luminaire with a frosted opal glass diffuser and thick ABS aerodynamically soundless blades.

    The Zephyr features a color-select switch which allows user to select between 2700K, 3000K, 4000K, and 5000K color temperature options.

    Sync the Zephyr with the exclusive Modern Forms app to control fan speed, and use smart features like adaptive learning, create groups, and more. Modern Forms fans pair with smart home tech including Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, Samsung Smart Things, Control 4, and Josh.ai.

    Whether you need a standard fit for regular rooms or a large ceiling fan for expansive spaces, this model is offered in multiple sizes to suit your layout. The 52 inch fan includes a 4.5 inch and 8 inch downrod. The 62 inch 72 inch fan includes an 8 inch downrod. All options include a Bluetooth F-RCBT remote control to control fan speed and light brightness, and switch between summer and winter mode. Optional wall switches available for 3 or 4 way setup, sold separately.

    Questions & Answers

    Ask a Question

    Can this fan be mounted on a sloped ceiling?

    January 9, 2021

    Answer:

    Yes, the fan itself has a ball in the canopy that allows for slopes up 30 degrees. For a ceiling pitch up to 12/12 (45 degree) the vendor makes a slope ceiling adapter kit.

    Does the integrated LED require bulb replacements?

    August 2, 2026

    Answer:

    No. The long-life LED is designed to minimize maintenance.

    Is the Zephyr compatible with smart home systems?

    April 5, 2026

    Answer:

    Yes. It integrates with popular smart home platforms for app and voice control.

    Can the color temperature be adjusted for different times of day?

    February 2, 2026

    Answer:

    Yes. The selectable lighting allows you to choose warmer or cooler light depending on your needs.

    Would this fan work well in a modern home office?

    February 1, 2026

    Answer:

    Yes. Its quiet operation and adjustable lighting make it a versatile workspace solution.

    Can I change the color temperature of the Zephyr Smart fan light?

    November 8, 2025

    Answer:

    Yes. The integrated LED features color-select technology that allows you to choose different color temperatures.

    Is the light designed to reduce glare?

    September 11, 2025

    Answer:

    Yes. The diffuser helps provide comfortable evenly distributed illumination.

    Can I adjust fan speeds remotely?

    May 12, 2025

    Answer:

    Yes. Multiple speed settings can be controlled through the app and included controls.

    Can I reverse the fan direction through the controls?

    March 26, 2025

    Answer:

    Yes. Reverse functionality is available through the control system.

    Does the integrated LED light dim?

    December 9, 2024

    Answer:

    Yes. The LED supports smooth dimming functionality.

    How energy efficient is the DC motor?

    June 12, 2024

    Answer:

    The DC motor helps reduce energy consumption while maintaining strong airflow.

    Can this fan be installed on a sloped ceiling?

    February 2, 2024

    Answer:

    Yes. It can accommodate many vaulted ceiling applications.

    What room size is best for the Zephyr?

    January 10, 2024

    Answer:

    Its efficient airflow makes it suitable for bedrooms living rooms and open-concept spaces.

    Does it have a replaceable bulb?

    April 21, 2023

    Answer:

    The light kit for this fixture is an integrated LED module which means it is built in, there is no bulb required. Generally LED modules like this will last 30,000-50,000 hours and through normal use, could last anywhere from 10-15 years. If you would like to request a new light kit please contact our sales department.

    Can I control the fan from my smartphone?

    February 5, 2023

    Answer:

    Yes. The fan can be operated through compatible mobile applications.

    Can this be installed outside, on a covered porch?

    July 25, 2021

    Answer:

    Yes, this fixture would be suitable for an outdoor covered porch as it is Wet Rated.

    Can this fan be installed in covered outdoor patios in hot, dry climates (Palm Springs)?

    March 5, 2021

    Answer:

    Yes! This fan will function normally in heat.

    Can the Zephyr Fan be flush mounted without a downrod?

    September 15, 2020

    Answer:

    The Zephyr Fan requires a downrod. The shortest one available is the 4.5 inch, and it is included with the fan. If you need something closer to the ceiling, you may want to search for a hugger fan.
