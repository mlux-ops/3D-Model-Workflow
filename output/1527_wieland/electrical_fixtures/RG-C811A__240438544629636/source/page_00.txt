Whisper Choice® DC Fan, Pick-A-Flow®, 80/110 CFM
Whisper Choice® DC Ventilation Fan, Pick-A-Flow®, 80/110 CFM
Model:
RG-C811A
This bathroom exhaust fan delivers powerful, energy-efficient ventilation to help improve air circulation, reduce excess moisture, and maintain a fresher indoor environment. Pick-A-Flow® technology allows airflow customization to match room size and ventilation needs, providing the ideal balance of comfort and performance. Designed for reliable, quiet operation, it helps support healthier indoor air quality and everyday comfort in bathrooms and other living spaces.

Key Features
Pick-A-Flow® selector lets you choose between 80 or 110 CFM with a quick flip of a switch
Extremely Quiet: with less than 0.4 sones at 80 CFM and 1.1 sones at 110 CFM. This fan is a great solution for outdated noisy fans
A housing depth of just 7-3/8", this is the slimmest ENERGY STAR® certified fan available, making it perfect for tight spaces and easy installation, even in 2" x 8" stud walls
Single-hinged Flex-Z-Fast® bracket provides flexible, fast and easy installation
Can be used to comply with ASHRAE 62.2, LEED®, IAP and California Title 24

Pick-A-Flow® Airflow selector - one fan, you choose the CFM
Built-in Pick-A-Flow® Airflow Selector provides the unique ability to select your required airflow of 80 or 110 CFM with the flip of a switch. Being able to choose the proper CFM airflow gives you flexibility and assurance that one fan will satisfy a range of ventilation design requirements and perform as promised, even with a complicated duct run.
Easy roomside installation for contractors and DIYers
Flex-Z Fast® installation bracket with articulating joints provides fast and trouble-free roomside installation from below the ceiling. The detachable installation adapter makes it easy to work with as it simply slides off the fan housing for effortless wiring and duct attachment, all from beneath the ceiling.
No rewiring or new wall switches needed. UL Listed® for tub and shower enclosures when GFCI protected.
Long life, lower cost of ownership
Panasonic fans use less energy to move larger volumes of air than other fans, and they run cooler so they last longer, providing efficient ventilation and healthy indoor air for many years. The built-in thermal-cutoff fuse practically eliminates chances of overheating which can result in motor failure, which means this may be the last vent fan you ever buy.
ENERGY STAR® certified for energy efficiency & savings
ENERGY STAR®-certified vent fans meet strict parameters set by the US Environmental Protection Agency. Panasonic ventilation fans are ENERGY STAR certified and provide greater efficiency and healthier indoor air quality with less noise. High performance motors and improved blade design deliver the superior performance and long-life operation you expect from a Panasonic ventilation fan.
Code Compliant: ENERGY STAR Certified, HVI (Home Ventilation Institute) Rated and complies with California Title 24 and Washington VIAQ codes and standards
