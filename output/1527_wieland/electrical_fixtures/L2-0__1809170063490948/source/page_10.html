The Sky Dome Flush Story

The Sky Collection is a modular pendant lighting system featuring multiple shade reflector solutions for various decorative and performance benefits. Sky Dome Flush is available in both spun aluminum and pressed wood shade reflectors that when combined with their powerful 2-sided LED light source gives the lamp a weightless appearance while providing unparalleled illumination in all directions. Sky Dome combined form and function with unsurpassed quality. At just 4" from the wall, it is ADA compliant for wall and corridor installations. Dome wood is available in 2 sizes: 18” (45.8cm) and 24”(61cm). Dome Metal is available in 3 sizes: 18” (45.8cm), 24”(61cm),  and 32” (81.3cm). Explore the rest of the Sky Collection with Sky Solo, Sky Dome, and Sky Sound.
 
Designer: Pablo Pardo + Pablo Studio 2021
 



Low Profile Brilliance

Direct + Indirect LightSky Dome Flush employs a bi-directional flat panel LED light source that provides up to 1250 lumens of glare free illumination and features Dim to Warm light technology that mimics nature's transformational light.

ADA CompliantAt just 4” from the wall, Sky Dome Flush is ADA compliant to illuminate your corridor path installations.

Refined MaterialitySky Dome shades reflectors are offered in a variety of handcrafted materials from pressed wood, matte painted aluminum, and brushed brass finishes to compliment any decor.




Sustainable

  • Ships flat-packed
  • Minimal use of materials
  • Contains no hazardous materials such as mercury
  • 97% of material is recyclable
  • Uses only 18W of power (90% more efficient than a comparable halogen light source, 40% more efficient than CFLs)
  • Designed for disassembly
  • Lasts for 50K hours or 25 years of daily use
.

Designers

Pablo Pardo
Venezuelan-born industrial designer Pablo Pardo has defined his career by creating beautiful, sparse, utilitarian lighting that fosters harmonious relationships between people and their environments.

Pablo Studio
The Pablo Studio is a small group of makers and inventors with a diverse background in industrial design and craft, and a deep understanding of manufacturing, materials and technology.
