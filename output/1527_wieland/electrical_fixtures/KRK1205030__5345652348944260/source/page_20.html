The Reed Wall Light delivers soft, ambient accent lighting for modern homes and sophisticated interiors. Inspired by slender, natural forms, these wall lights complement the Reed portable luminaires, offering versatile placement and a distinctive aesthetic.

Precision-engineered from aluminium and brass, each light is hand-finished and available in electroplated or powder-coated options, combining durability with sophisticated style. Perfect for feature walls, hallways, or living spaces seeking contemporary, high-end lighting solutions. 

Offered with an outward or inward-facing LED.
