CASTLE MUSE Pendant
Derived from Greek mythology, the Muses were nine goddesses who symbolized the highest achievements in art and science. Today, a muse is known as a person or object that inspires creativity. At SEED Design, we’ve captured that spark of ingenuity in our own creation—the CASTLE MUSE Pendant.
Crafted from a unique fusion of opal glass and concrete, this pendant is stunning in its simplicity. Available in both classic grey concrete and a deep, dramatic “Noir,” each version brings its own distinct personality and presence to any space.
