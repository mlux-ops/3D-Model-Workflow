Hanging Harware is Complete Garbage
By Steve
Sep 07, 2019
In order to hang this, you have to attach a bracket to the ceiling and then the light slides into it. Well, this is a very poor design. I attached the bracket to my ceiling and the light just will not slide into the bracket. I spend well over 5 hours on this. It turned out that the tabs on the light fixture were hitting the ceiling and digging in so I could not slide the light. I ended up unscrewing all four tabs from the fixture and bending them so they were straighter and could slide into the slots on the bracket without hitting the ceiling. I managed to get two of the tabs to slide into the bracket, but I just can't get the other two to engage. It's hanging my only half of the tabs, but I am done with it. I wasted too much time. Also, the safety wire does not stay in the slot. What a piece of garbage. If I hadn't already drilled 1/4" hols in my ceiling, I would return this. However, there was no way to tell this would happen until the bracket was installed onto my ceiling. I could never recommend this light to anyone, even an enemy.
