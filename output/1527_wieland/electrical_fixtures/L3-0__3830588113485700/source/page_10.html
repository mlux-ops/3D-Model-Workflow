The Sky Dome Story

The Sky Collection is a modular pendant lighting system featuring multiple shade reflector solutions for various decorative and performance benefits. Sky Dome is available in both spun aluminum (matte black, matte white, brushed brass) and pressed wood (white oak or walnut) shade reflectors that when combined with their powerful 2-sided LED light source gives the lamp a weightless appearance while providing unparalleled illumination in all directions.  Sky Dome combines form and function with unsurpassed quality and is available in 3 sizes :  18”(45.8cm)/24”(61cm)/32"(81cm). Explore the rest of the Sky Collection with Sky Solo, Sky Sound and our latest addition Sky Dome Flush.

Direct + Indirect LightThe Sky Series utilizes a two-sided flat panel light source.
It provides up to 1250 lumens of dimmable warm light
in all directions.

Material StoryThe Sky Dome is available in both spun aluminum and
pressed wood shades that give each lamp varying
aesthetic properties and reflect light in unique ways.

Mimics Natural LightThe Sky Series features Dim-to-Warm Technology to provide a natural range (2700K-2200K) of warm, dimmable light in all directions.

The Sky Dome employs a range of materials that are designed to bring a premium flair to residential, contract, and hospitality settings alike.

Meet Sky Dome.

.

Sustainable

  • Ships flat-packed
  • Minimal use of materials
  • Contains no hazardous materials such as mercury
  • 97% of material is recyclable
  • Uses only 18W of power (90% more efficient than a comparable halogen light source, 40% more efficient than CFLs)
  • Designed for disassembly
  • Lasts for 50K hours or 25 years of daily use
.

Designers

Pablo Pardo
Venezuelan-born industrial designer Pablo Pardo has defined his career by creating beautiful, sparse, utilitarian lighting that fosters harmonious relationships between people and their environments.

Pablo Studio
The Pablo Studio is a small group of makers and inventors with a diverse background in industrial design and craft, and a deep understanding of manufacturing, materials and technology.
