The Sky Dome Story
The Sky Collection is a modular pendant lighting system featuring multiple shade reflector solutions for various decorative and performance benefits. Sky Dome is available in both spun aluminum (matte black, matte white, brushed brass) and pressed wood (white oak or walnut) shade reflectors that when combined with their powerful 2-sided LED light source gives the lamp a weightless appearance while providing unparalleled illumination in all directions. Sky Dome combines form and function with unsurpassed quality and is available in 3 sizes : 18”(45.8cm)/24”(61cm)/32"(81cm). Explore the rest of the Sky Collection with
Sky Solo
,
Sky Sound
and our latest addition
Sky Dome Flush
.

Direct + Indirect Light
The Sky Series utilizes a two-sided flat panel light source.
It provides up to 1250 lumens of dimmable warm light
in all directions.
Material Story
The Sky Dome is available in both spun aluminum and
pressed wood shades that give each lamp varying
aesthetic properties and reflect light in unique ways.
Mimics Natural Light
The Sky Series features Dim-to-Warm Technology to provide a natural range (2700K-2200K) of warm, dimmable light in all directions.
The Sky Dome employs a range of materials that are designed to bring a premium flair to residential, contract, and hospitality settings alike.
