The Milan Bathroom Vanity Light shows off crisp lines in its elegant rectilinear form accented by a discreet metal band, elevating any contemporary bathroom or powder room. 24.5 inch light (120V): TRIAC or ELV dimmable. 36.5 inch light (120-277V): TRIAC, ELV, or 0-10V dimmable.
The Natural Brass is a living finish that will patina and warm over time, enhancing its richness. If the patina becomes uneven or spotty, a brass cleaner can restore the finish.
