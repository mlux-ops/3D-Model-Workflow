QuickShip Terms & Conditions

QuickShip Purchase Orders must be designated as such and placed separately from standard Purchase Orders.
  • Available color and finish options vary per product, refer to individual product pages.
  • All QuickShip orders must be submitted online at SkLO.com
  • SkLO ships within 10 business days from your order confirmation and receipt of payment in full (net terms apply). QuickShip lead times do not include transit time. Transit times vary by destination.
  • SkLO is not responsible for the transit times of 3rd party shipping companies.
  • Standard Shipping Policy and Terms & Conditions apply.

Quote

Use Quote to collect products within a single project document that can be shared with your client for review and/or revision. When you’re ready to place your order, Quote allows you to convert directly to a Sales Order.
  • Add products to Quote directly from product pages by selecting your desired finishes and specifications.
  • Review all selected items in one place including variants, finishes, dimensions, and quantities. Quote will include subtotals based on selected pricing mode.
  • Perform revisions on existing quotes – add and remove products, update quantities, adjust pricing mode or project details.
  • Title your project and include your client’s email for sending Quote to your client.
  • Save Quote to your account for access to review and revise at your convenience, download to PDF, or convert directly to a sales order.

An expressive glass vessel that appears to droop from its hook as if molten serves as a shade on this wall sconce. Every Hold glass piece is shaped by hand. Subtle variations in wall thickness and color density mean no two pieces are ever identical. The socket is suspended from the hook on a fabric-wrapped electrical cord and is loosely suspended within the glass. A removable threaded pin within the brass hook can be used to secure the glass if required. Available in two sizes.

About Hold Sconce

The craft of glassblowing

The simple gesture of the glass element of the Hold Sconce, appearing to stretch from its hook, is meant as a pure expression of the craft of glassblowing. Each one is created, one at a time, entirely by hand, by skilled Czech glassblowers. Intentionally different every time, no two are alike. When used in groups, the differences become subtly clear, giving individuality to each one, and an organic composition to the group.

SkLO classic

The Hold Sconce is a perfect statement piece for any wall light location. Its two sizes make it flexible enough to fit within different spaces. A classic SkLO design, it is functional enough to work in bathrooms, fantastic as bedside lights, and perfect in hospitality projects large and small.
