700BCTRS3C-LED930
Tris 3-Light Bath 3 Light Chrome LED 90 CRI 3000K 120V
Tris by Sean Lavin reflects simple geometric aesthetics that define this modern 3-light bath vanity fixture. Integrated LED light guide technology appears as frosted glass when off then illuminates across its surface when turned on. Spanning 25 in. in length and offered modern finishes, the Tris bath bar is a great way to light your bathroom or vanity. Lamping options are compatible with most dimmers. Refer to the Dimming Chart for more information.
