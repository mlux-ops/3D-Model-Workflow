MSI's Warm whites and creams come together on a beautiful porcelain surface to create the stunning Soreno Ivory floor and wall tiles. Warm neutrals are trendy flooring surfaces as they can make a space look and feel more extensive, and the colors complement any architectural style and design palette. Use Soreno Ivory tile for added design flexibility. Extend your space beyond four walls and into an outdoor oasis with the Soreno Ivory tiles. Use these attractive surface solutions in the kitchen, living room, bedroom, hallway, or on a patio, walkway, or around a pool. Durable, highly resistant, and easy to maintain, Soreno Ivory is the perfect way to showcase your style indoors and outdoor.

This Soreno Ivory Matte Porcelain Floor And Wall Tile - MSI Collection offers;

  • Grade 1, first-quality porcelain tile for floor and wall use.
  • Available in 24"x24" and 24"x48" sizes.
  • Glazed smooth finish with a low sheen and slight variation in tone.
  • Impervious flooring has water absorption of less than 0.5% for indoor or some outdoor use and is frost proof.
  • Completely frost resistant for indoor or outdoor applications; use a latex-modified thinset for acceptable bond strength.
  • This is a GREENGUARD Indoor Air Quality Certified and GREENGUARD Children and Schools Certified product.

Damages and issues
