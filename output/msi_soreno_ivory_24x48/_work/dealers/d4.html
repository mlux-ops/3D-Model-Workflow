MSI Soreno Ivory 24" x 48" Matte Porcelain Tile Premium (16.00 sqft/each)

Warm whites and creams come together on a beautiful porcelain surface to create the stunning Soreno Ivory pavers and tiles. Warm neutrals are trendy flooring surfaces as they can make a space look and feel more extensive, and the colors complement any architectural style and design palette. The Soreno Ivory mosaic tile is available in 24x24, 24x48, and 2x2 sizes, while the pavers are available in 2cm, 24x24, 24x48, and 13x24 pool coping for added design flexibility. Extend your space beyond four walls and into an outdoor oasis with the Soreno Ivory tiles and pavers. Use these attractive surface solutions in the kitchen, living room, bedroom, hallway, or on a patio, walkway, or around a pool. Durable, highly resistant, and easy to maintain, Soreno Ivory is the perfect way to showcase your style indoors and out.
