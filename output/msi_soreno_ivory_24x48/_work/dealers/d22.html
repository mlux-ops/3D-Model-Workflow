              Description
              • Durable porcelain material ensures long-lasting outdoor beauty
              • Matte finish offers a contemporary, stylish appearance
              • Versatile size perfect for patios and backyards
              Create a touch of elegance to your exteriors with Soreno Ivory porcelain pavers from the Arterra Collection. With creamy white tones and hues, these pavers come in two large format sizes including 24x24 and 24x48, making them the perfect choice for creating a welcoming patio, pool surround or outdoor walkway. With a high load bearing capacity, these pavers offer maximum strength and durability so they can be applied to sand-set paving driveways and other areas with high foot traffic. They have a matte finish and natural stone look, making them easy to maintain and clean. Soreno Ivory is great in a single layout or group, so you can create a hardscaping oasis from the front patio to the backyard and beyond.
