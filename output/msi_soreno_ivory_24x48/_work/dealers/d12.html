    Soreno Indoor & Outdoor Tile Collection

    The Soreno Collection achieves the sought-after look of limestone in an easy-care, durable tile. Offered in neutral warm colorways that set the stage for nearly any design scheme, this indoor/outdoor tile collection creates livable spaces that sustain the test of time. Soreno is available in numerous sizes and options in both porcelain tile and Arterra® Porcelain Pavers for maximum versatility. Bring a luxurious, family friendly space to life with Soreno.
