Warm whites and creams come together on a beautiful porcelain surface to create the stunning Soreno Ivory floor and wall tiles. Warm neutrals are trendy flooring surfaces as they can make a space look and feel more extensive, and the colors complement any architectural style and design palette. Use Soreno Ivory tile for added design flexibility. Extend your space beyond four walls and into an outdoor oasis with the Soreno Ivory tiles. Use these attractive surface solutions in the kitchen, living room, bedroom, hallway, or on a patio, walkway, or around a pool. Durable, highly resistant, and easy to maintain, Soreno Ivory is the perfect way to showcase your style indoors and outdoor
