# QuietMount screwless — five holders

Open QuietMount_Screwless_H2D_READY.3mf as a project in Bambu Studio. It contains two arranged plates, printer/material/process settings and sliced toolpaths for an H2D with standard-flow 0.4 mm nozzles, Textured PEI and PolyTerra PLA. Built using Bambu Studio 02.08.00.50. Select your actual spool in the print dialog.

Print both plates once. There are 20 parts: five adhesive wall plates, five frames with integrated lower hooks, five top hooks and five locking keys. No screws, nuts, nails or glue are needed for assembly.

## Print

Plate 1: adhesive wall plates, top hooks and keys. Plate 2: frames. Preserve orientations and supports. Adhesive pads face upward and receive topmost-surface ironing; the textured bed finish belongs on the opposite face. Frames and top hooks use supports around hidden features. Carefully remove supports from the mounting buttons, guide, and locking-key slots.

Quality profile: 0.08 mm layers, 0.20 mm first layer, six walls, 25 mm/s outer walls, 220°C first-layer nozzle / 215°C thereafter, 55°C bed, 0.98 flow baseline and 8 mm³/s volumetric cap. Fine layers and ironing make this a long print, approximately 41 hours and 242 g across both plates; final labels may slightly change the estimate. Use Preview for the current estimate. Process and filament JSON presets are included separately; the project already embeds them.

Bambu retains its PLA bed-temperature/enclosure advisory at 55°C. Ventilate the enclosure for PLA. Spool-specific flow calibration can improve fit and appearance; re-slice if you change the profile.

## Assemble and mount

1. Dry-fit before applying adhesive. Hold the wall plate with its smooth raised pads facing the wall and the wide ends of its two central openings ABOVE their narrow tails.
2. Insert the frame's two rear mounting buttons through those wide openings with the frame raised approximately 10 mm. Slide the frame down fully. Its button heads engage behind the wall plate. The frame should resist an outward pull while remaining removable by lifting it 10 mm.
3. Slide the matching top hook down over the frame's central T guide until it rests on the stop. Insert the printed locking key from the right, its tall handle pointing upward behind the sample. Seat it gently until snug. The tapered end provides a small friction fit; do not hammer it or force tight joints. Remove residual support material first.
4. Remove the frame from the wall plate. Fit two matched pairs of **3M Command Medium Picture Hanging Strips, model 17201**, vertically on the raised pads. Each pad is 22 × 74 mm. Keep the stretch-release tabs accessible at the bottom; do not trim the strips. Confirm your actual strips fit entirely on the pads before applying them.
5. Follow the strip package instructions: clean the smooth, sound painted wall with rubbing alcohol, allow it to dry, apply pressure as directed, and wait at least one hour before loading. Newly painted walls need at least seven days of curing. The two-pair recommendation requires 10 pairs / 20 individual strips for all five holders.
6. After the adhesive has set, slide the frame onto its wall plate. Withdraw the locking key toward the right, hold the frame down, and lift or remove the top hook. Set the sample onto the lower hooks, against the rear pads. Lower the top hook onto its stop and reinsert the key. The top hook retains the sample; it should not squeeze it.
7. To remove a sample, support it, hold the frame down, withdraw the key and raise the top hook at least 6 mm. Tip the sample forward and lift it off the lower hooks. Avoid levering against the adhesive mount. To access the strip tabs, remove the sample and frame, then separate the wall plate from the paired strips as directed by 3M. Stretch adhesive tabs slowly downward along the wall, never outward.

The rear sample plane is approximately 18 mm from the wall with the illustrated strip thickness; actual strip thickness varies. Only three narrow front tabs overlap the sample face. Lower hooks are integrated into the frame, so changing thickness requires a different frame and top hook.

## Verification status

Digital geometry checks covered all four thicknesses, slide motion, sample clearance, frame capture and key retention. Earlier unlabelled meshes passed independent STL checks and the project sliced successfully. The final labelled project was refreshed, but additional final checks were skipped at your request. No physical print, adhesive-bond test or sustained-load test has been performed. Treat this as an untested printable prototype and test one holder before displaying the terrazzo sample. A Command strip rating does not establish a load rating for a printed plastic mount or its paint bond.

The approximately 1 lb terrazzo estimate comes from 10 lb/ft² × (3.75 × 3.75)/144. Printed surface adhesion remains unverified even with ironing; do not load a pad with gaps, ridges or loose surface material.
